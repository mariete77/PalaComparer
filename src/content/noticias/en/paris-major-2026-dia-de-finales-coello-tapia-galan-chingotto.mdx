The Alpine Paris Major closes today on Philippe Chatrier with a card that needs no embellishment: **Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia** can lift their **fourth consecutive title at Roland-Garros** this afternoon, and standing across the net will be the very pair they beat in the **2025 final**: **Ale Galán and Fede Chingotto**, champions of the Madrid P1 exactly one week ago. Before that, the women's final pits an **Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero** duo that came back from a set down in the semis against **Claudia Fernández and Martina Calvo**, the pair that has just done the unthinkable: beating the world No. 1s three times in a row.

## The semi-finals: the No. 1s show no mercy, but the underdogs don't either

Saturday delivered history in equal shares.

- **Ari Sánchez / Andrea Ustero** d. Marta Ortega / Sofía Araújo **5-7, 6-4, 6-1**. They came back against the pair that had saved five match points against Josemaría-González on Friday in over three hours. It is their **first Major final together**.
- **Martina Calvo / Claudia Fernández** d. Gemma Triay / Delfi Brea **6-4, 6-2**. Third consecutive win over the No. 1s: the defending champions fall one step short of the final they won last year.
- **Agustín Tapia / Arturo Coello** d. Juan Lebrón / Leo Augsburger **7-5, 6-3**, in 1h07. Lebrón had his chances in the first set, but the No. 1s closed out the big points the way they have been doing in Paris since 2023.
- **Ale Galán / Fede Chingotto** d. Martín Di Nenno / Paquito Navarro **7-5, 6-2**. The rematch of the Madrid duel went Galán-Chingotto's way again, after Di Nenno-Paquito had needed an epic comeback to survive the quarters (4-6, 6-1, 6-4 over Stupaczuk-Sanz).

## The men's final: four in a row against revenge

If Coello and Tapia win today, they will string together **four straight Paris Majors** (2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026), a dominance no other duo has managed in the tournament's history. But context demands caution: they lost to Yanguas-Nieto at the Madrid P1, and Galán-Chingotto arrive in Paris with the **Movistar Arena title and seven titles this season**, the hottest pair on tour. Today is also the **exact replay of the 2025 final**, when Coello and Tapia dominated Galán and Chingotto on Chatrier: revenge could not ask for a better stage.

## The women's final: the most unexpected of the year

Nobody had Fernández-Calvo in the final when the week began, yet they are now the pair that knows Triay-Brea's weaknesses best. Across the net, Sánchez-Ustero chase their first Major title together with Ari's adidas Arrow in comeback mode. Delfi Brea, eliminated, leaves the Vertex 05 Woman in the display case; the final belongs to two hungry pairs.
