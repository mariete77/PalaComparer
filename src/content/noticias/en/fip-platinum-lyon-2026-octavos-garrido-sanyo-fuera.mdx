The **FIP Platinum Lyon 2026 plays its round of 16 this Thursday, September 24**: 16 matches (8 men's and 8 women's) from **11:00 (French time)** across Centre Court Ville de Lyon and Courts 2 and 3 at the Palais des Sports de Gerland and the All In Country Academy. After Wednesday's first round, **the top four seeds in each draw make their debut today** following their byes: among the men, Stupaczuk-Sanz, Leal-Guerrero, Di Nenno-Tello and Momo-Campagnolo; among the women, Salazar-Osoro, Alonso-Dal Pozzo, Caldera-Goenaga and Rufo-Castello. (Sources: official order of play for September 24, padelmagazine.fr; results, studypadel.com and the official draws at padelfip.com.)

## The upset of the first round: Axelsson-Guichard send Garrido-Sanyo packing

The match that opened the campaign of the No. 5 seeds ended in elimination: **Javier Garrido and Sanyo Gutierrez fell to the home pair Adam Axelsson-Dylan Guichard 3-6 6-4 6-4** on Centre Court. The Spanish pair lost their grip after taking the opening set, and with them went the two catalog rackets that starred in the preview: **Garrido's Wilson Defy Pro V1** — <ProductRef id="wilson-defy-pro-v1-2026" /> — and **Sanyo's StarVie Eternal 2027** — <ProductRef id="starvie-eternal-2027" /> —, with their [player pages](/jugadores/javi-garrido) and [Sanyo's](/jugadores/sanyo-gutierrez) still live on PalaComparer. Axelsson-Guichard, meanwhile, are enjoying their best week and today face **Montiel-Santigosa**, who beat the French pair Boronad-Robert 6-2 6-3.

## The men's first round, result by result

- **Esbri-Jofre d. Aragon-Roglan 6-3 6-1** — the 2025 champion is still alive in Lyon: today they meet **Oria-Ortega**.
- **Abbate-Rodriguez d. Fonteny-Moragues (WC) 6-4 6-4** — they will be the opening exam for **Stupaczuk-Sanz**.
- **Rubini-Chozas d. Quilez-Mourino 6-3 7-6** and **Ruiz-Gonzalez Blanco d. Zapata-Naranjo 6-2 7-5** — they meet each other in the round of 16.
- **Hernandez-Castano d. Garcia-Patiniotis 6-4 6-7 6-3** — their reward is a clash with **Di Nenno-Tello**.
- **Goenaga-Collado d. Sanchez-Garcia Rodrigo 6-3 6-4** — they face **Momo-Campagnolo**.
- **Moya-Leygue d. Lamperti-Alvarez 6-7 7-6 6-2** — the comeback that closed the day (it finished late, which is why the order of play had not yet reflected it) earns them a meeting with **Edu Alonso-Aimar Goni**.

## The French women lead the first round

- **Mansart-Dardaine (WC) d. Santamaria-Foguer 7-6(5) 6-4** — the French wildcards delivered the home performance of the day and today face the No. 2 seeds, **Alonso-Dal Pozzo**.
- **Collombon-Sharifova d. Alonso De Lera-Dionisio 2-6 6-1 6-3** — a comeback by the No. 7 seeds: today against **Val Lopez-Bahurel** (6-4 6-3 over Sandu-Marcus).
- **Marchetti-Arruabarrena d. Perez Parra-Guryanova 6-1 6-0** — the No. 8 seeds did not drop a single game in the second set: today against **Morinigo-Martin** (6-4 6-0).
- **Fassio-Perez d. Betz-Groenveld 6-1 6-1** and **Valenzuela-Godallier d. Sanchez Perez-Fernandez 6-2 6-3** — they face each other today.
- **Sainz-Manquillo d. Pothier-Robinson 6-1 6-1** — today against **Vano-Castillon** (6-2 6-4).
- **Guimet-Ryzhova d. Eugenio-Sanchez 6-3 6-1** — the first obstacle for **Salazar-Osoro** in their title defence.
- **Aguilar-Escacena d. Portilla-Ordonez 6-3 6-2** — today against **Rufo-Castello**.

## The round of 16 with catalog rackets on court

**Stupaczuk-Sanz vs Abbate-Rodriguez** (Centre Court, not before 3:30 PM). The No. 1 seeds open their campaign with both catalog rackets on court: the **Siux Electra Pro IT 26** — <ProductRef id="siux-electra-pro-it-26-2026" /> — and the **Drop Shot Axion Attack 2.0 2026** — <ProductRef id="drop-shot-axion-attack-2-0-2026" /> —, with the [player pages of Stupa](/jugadores/franco-stupaczuk) and [Jon Sanz](/jugadores/jon-sanz) already in the catalog. Stupa knows the way: this year he won the Platinum in Tirana alongside Yanguas and the FIP Gold in Belgrade with Sanz.

**Hernandez-Castano vs Di Nenno-Tello** (Centre Court, not before 1:00 PM). Juan Tello takes to the court with his **Bullpadel Vertex 05 2026** — <ProductRef id="bullpadel-vertex-05-2026" /> —, with his [player page](/jugadores/juan-tello) live.

**Momo-Campagnolo vs Goenaga-Collado**. Momo Gonzalez plays with the **Wilson Endure Pro V1** — <ProductRef id="wilson-endure-pro-v1-2026" /> — on his [player page](/jugadores/momo-gonzalez).

**Salazar-Osoro vs Guimet-Ryzhova**. The title defence of **Alejandra Salazar** — in her final year as a pro — starts this afternoon with the **Bullpadel Flow Woman** — <ProductRef id="bullpadel-flow-2024" /> — in hand, with her [player page](/jugadores/alejandra-salazar) in the catalog.
