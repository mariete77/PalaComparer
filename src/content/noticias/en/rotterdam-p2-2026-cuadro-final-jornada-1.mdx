The **CUPRA Rotterdam Premier Padel P2** (Rotterdam Ahoy, **September 27 – October 4**, €264,534 in prize money and 500 FIP points for the champions) opens its **main draw** this afternoon: at **18:30** — no time difference with Spain — the men's round of 32 begins with four matches, after a morning that settles the last spots in qualifying. The tournament is played indoors, across four courts set up in the Rotterdam venue.

## Lamperti, one win from the draw in his farewell tour

The morning (from 12:00) resolves the **men's Q3** and the **women's Q2** — the four men's winners complete the qualified spots of the draw. The name of the day in qualifying is **Miguel Lamperti**: at **47 years old** (born November 11, 1978) and on his announced 2026 farewell tour, the Argentine, **No. 111 in the world**, is **one match away from the main draw**: he and Octavio Álvarez (seventh seeds in qualifying) take on Zapata-Naranjo (04).

The other Q3 matchups: **Roglan Pons-Aragón Herrera (01)** against E. Mouriño-Navarro Porras, **Ortega-Axelsson (02)** against **Insa Sotillo-Miñano Ortinez (08)** — who won their Q2 yesterday with a commanding 6-4, 6-1 — and the local pair **Roper-Släryd** (Thijs Roper plays at home) against Moragues Moltó-Fonteny (05), also winners yesterday (6-4, 7-6). On the women's side, the Q2 leaves two Dutch pairs, **Hemmes-Luttikhuis** and **Groenveld-Betz**, one win away from the draw: a good barometer of the local padel the tournament wants to celebrate.

## The afternoon: four matchups with a catalog flavour (18:30)

- **Goñi-Alonso vs Montiel-Santigosa** (Center Court): the breakout pair of the Paris Major — quarter-finalists after knocking out Yanguas-Nieto — make their Rotterdam debut with **Aimar Goñi freshly selected for Spain** ahead of the FIP World Cup in Doha (November 2-7). Edu Alonso plays with his <ProductRef id="nox-ea10-ventus-attack-12k-2026" />. The winner meets Tapia-Coello in the last 16.
- **Rodríguez Martínez-Abbate vs Libaak-Alfonso**: two of the season's breakout pairs go head to head. Abbate-Rodríguez were the surprise of the Madrid P1 and already won at the start of Lyon; **Libaak-Alfonso** are the pair that knocked out Lebrón-Augsburger in the Madrid P1 round of 16, and Tino plays with the <ProductRef id="siux-diablo-pro-2026" />.
- **Aguirre-García Rodrigo vs Tello-Arce**: the partner shuffle goes on. **Juan Tello**, who played the FIP Platinum Lyon with Di Nenno, lines up in Rotterdam alongside Maxi Arce, with the <ProductRef id="bullpadel-vertex-05-2026" /> as the catalog reference.
- **Lijó-Gil vs Quilez-Mouriño** (Court 2) wraps up the afternoon session.

## The seeds wait: Di Nenno-Paquito debut their partnership

The official draw — released on Sunday by the tournament direction — gives the **top four seeds a bye straight to the last 16**: Tapia-Coello (01), Galán-Chingotto (02), Augsburger-Lebrón (03) and the tournament's novelty: **Di Nenno and Paquito Navarro (04) play together in Rotterdam**, with 11,346 FIP ranking points between the two of them. Paquito's Hack 05 is in the catalog.

Wednesday (12:00) completes the men's first round: **Stupa-Sanz (05)** — runners-up in Lyon on Sunday — against a qualifier, **Yanguas-Nieto (06)** versus Rubio-Fernández Lancha, FIP Platinum Lyon champions **Leal-Guerrero (07)** against Goenaga-Collado and **González-Campagnolo (08)** versus Hernández Quesada-Javier Martínez, plus the two local wild cards (Prins-de Kroon and Richters-Olsson).

## The women's bracket starts tomorrow: all eight seeds rest

The eight seeded pairs in the women's bracket rest in the first round: Triay-Brea (01), Josemaría-González (02), Sánchez-Ustero (03), Fernández-Calvo (04), Ortega-Araújo (05), Guinart-Alonso de Villa (06), Salazar-Osoro (07) and Jensen-Escacena (08). The eight round-of-32 matches are played tomorrow; among them, Dutch wild cards **Koek-Weterings** against Talaván-Castelló and **Sharifova-Collombon** versus a qualifier stand out.
