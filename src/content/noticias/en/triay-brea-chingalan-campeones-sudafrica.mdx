The first Premier Padel tournament ever held in South Africa has its champions: **Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea** in the women's draw and **Alejandro Galán and Federico Chingotto** in the men's. The world No.1 women's pair and the No.2 men's duo took the title in Pretoria after two nail-biting finals, both decided in a third-set tie-break.

## Two finals that almost saw upsets

The two pairs playing their first-ever Premier Padel final came agonisingly close to the upset. **Martina Calvo and Claudia Fernández**, in their first tournament together, recovered from a set down before falling 6-2, 6(5)-7, 7-6(4) to Triay and Brea. In the men's draw, **Javi Leal and Fran Guerrero** — regular CUPRA FIP Tour title winners — went down 6-2, 5-7, 7-6(7) against Galán-Chingotto.

<Callout title="Tournament facts">
The SunBet Arena in Pretoria hosted two finals lasting over three hours. Experience and pedigree were the difference in the decisive moments.
</Callout>

## Season numbers

With this win, Triay and Brea add **their fifth title of the season** and extend their lead at the top of the FIP rankings. For Galán and Chingotto it's the **sixth trophy of the year**, after a run of five consecutive tournaments won by world No.1s Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia.

## The tour moves on: London debuts

From Pretoria the tour heads straight to **London**, where a Premier Padel tournament will be held for the first time in the British capital. The **London P1** main draw gets underway at the iconic Olympia London (Kensington) with the world's best players in action.
