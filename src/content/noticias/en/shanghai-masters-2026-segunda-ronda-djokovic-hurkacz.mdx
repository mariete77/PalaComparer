The **Rolex Shanghai Masters** moves into its **second round** this Friday — the tournament runs from 7 to 18 October — with a host of the big favourites starting their campaigns. Today the top seed, **Alexander Zverev**, American **Ben Shelton** (3) and, in the Stadium Court night session, **Novak Djokovic** all open their tournaments, with the Serb taking on fellow former champion **Hubert Hurkacz**. The second round is split across Friday and Saturday; the rest of the contenders — Alcaraz, Medvedev, Fritz, Tsitsipas — already know their opponents.

## Djokovic-Hurkacz, a champions' duel

Djokovic arrived in Shanghai with the best possible momentum: **his 102nd ATP title in Beijing**, "the best week of this year, no doubt," as he put it. "I'm trying to build momentum on that here in Shanghai as well, use the confidence and hopefully have another great deep run in this tournament," he said in his pre-tournament press conference, in which he also paid tribute to **Kei Nishikori**, who ended his career last week in Tokyo. The 39-year-old Serb is the tournament's most decorated player — **four titles, a record** — and competed in the event's very first edition back in 2009.

Across the net he will find a familiar face: **Hurkacz**, champion in 2023, is back in Shanghai for the first time since, and in form — a final in Chengdu, semi-finals in Beijing, where he retired with adductor trouble he says is now behind him. On Thursday he fired **19 aces** to see off James Duckworth in 81 minutes (6-3, 7-6(4)). "It's going to be a fun challenge," he said of Djokovic, whom he has **never beaten: 0-8 in their head-to-head**. "I need to serve well, play well from the baseline and return well. It's always a pleasure to share the court with Novak, such a great champion."

## Zverev and Shelton also begin

The top seed needs no introduction: **Zverev** arrives with this year's Grand Slam double (Roland Garros and the US Open), a 58-15 record and **on the brink of becoming the 30th No. 1 in ATP Rankings history** (since 1973). Already qualified for the Nitto ATP Finals, he is chasing his third title of the season; he faces wild card **Yibing Wu**, who delighted the home crowd by beating lucky loser Michael Zheng 6-3, 7-6(6). "I have big motivation," said the German. "The only thing I can control is what I do on the tennis court and my next match."

**Shelton** (3), the US Open runner-up, opens against Germany's **Daniel Altmaier**, the man who knocked out Rune. And the night session closes with another local favourite: **Yi Zhou** — a winner over Mattia Bellucci — takes on Italy's **Lorenzo Musetti** (24).

## First round: Rune's fall, Tsitsipas' revival

The biggest name already out is **Holger Rune**. The Dane, back on tour a few weeks ago after **almost a year out with a ruptured Achilles**, lost to **Daniel Altmaier** (1-6, 7-6(5), 6-4) in just his second tournament since returning. **Carlos Alcaraz**, who practised with him in Murcia in August, sent a message of support: "It's a slow process. The injury he had, it's a really complicated one... I'm really happy to see him back on court. He has to be patient."

The opposite story belongs to **Stefanos Tsitsipas**. The Greek had to **come through qualifying** and on Thursday beat Kimmer Coppejans (6-3, 7-5) to keep a striking record intact: **he has never lost his opening match in Shanghai (6-0)**. Up to No. 38 in the live rankings, he already has a second-round opponent: 18th seed Luciano Darderi, on Saturday.

Other first-round notes:

- **Berrettini**, with **23 aces and without facing a single break point**, came from a set down against Aleksandar Kovacevic (6-7(3), 6-1, 6-4). Today he meets Nakashima (16) on Show Court 3.
- **Hanfmann** took the marathon against Majchrzak in **three tie-breaks** (6-7(4), 7-6(3), 7-6(7)). Today's reward: Tiafoe (7).
- **Arnaldi** blitzed Bernard Tomic in 52 minutes (6-3, 6-1), the Australian back in a Masters 1000 main draw; the Italian gets Fritz (11) on Saturday, with the American 12th in the Race to Turin.
- **J.M. Cerundolo** (6-4, 6-4 over Mejia) and **Struff** (6-4, 6-4 over Landaluce) also won and now await Alcaraz and Medvedev respectively.
