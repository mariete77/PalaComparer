If Wednesday's round of 16 was the earthquake with the fall of Lebrón-Augsburger, quarter-final day at the **Kuwait Major** confirmed the season's skin is changing. **Tino Libaak and Gonzalo Alfonso** knocked out **Martín Di Nenno and Paquito Navarro (5)** 7-6(5), 6-1 —their second big-name victim in 48 hours— and this afternoon the semi-finals are played (from 12:00 Spanish time on Red Bull TV) with three pairs debuting at this stage of a Major. In the women's draw, the familiar script: the top four seeds in the semis for the fourth consecutive tournament.

## Libaak and Alfonso: the week of their lives

The official Premier Padel report captures the fine detail: the Argentines came back from 2-5 down in the first-set tie-break, and from there the rollercoaster only went one way, with a crushing 6-1 closing set. It is their first Major semi-final and only the second they play together after the Madrid P1; the result guarantees them at least 780 FIP Race points and brings them closer to the Finals at the Palau Sant Jordi. For Gonzalo Alfonso there is a personal footnote: his first win over Paquito Navarro in four meetings. And there is no rest in sight: next week they defend Argentina's colours at the FIP World Cup, alongside Maxi Arce. Libaak keeps doing it with the <ProductRef id="siux-diablo-pro-2026" /> in his hand.

## Coello-Tapia: routine, mathematics and a fifth date with the upset-makers

**Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia (1)** dispatched Jairo Bautista and Lucas Bergamini (11) 6-1, 6-4 and keep intact the outlook they landed in Kuwait with: reach the final and year-end No. 1 for a fourth consecutive season is all but theirs. In between stands the fifth meeting with Libaak-Alfonso. Tapia plays the <ProductRef id="nox-at10-genius-18k-2026" /> and Coello the <ProductRef id="head-coello-pro-2026" />.

## In the other semi, a debutant finalist guaranteed

The bottom half of the draw serves up **Juan Tello and Maxi Arce (9)** against **Edu Alonso and Aimar Goñi (11)**, and with it one certainty: Saturday will feature a first-time finalist pair. Tello-Arce ended the dream of [Sintes and Zamora](/en/noticias/kuwait-major-2026-sintes-zamora-sorpresa) —the lowest-ranked pair ever to play a Major quarter-final— with a 6-4, 6-2 win in one hour and seven minutes. For Tello it is a first Major semi-final since Paris 2022, when he lost the final alongside Chingotto; for Arce, the first of his career. The performance of the day belonged to Alonso-Goñi: 6-1, 6-2 over **Stupaczuk-Sanz (4)** in 54 minutes, the shortest match of the day, and a first semi-final since Asunción in May. Edu Alonso competes with the <ProductRef id="nox-ea10-ventus-attack-12k-2026" />, Tello with the <ProductRef id="bullpadel-vertex-05-2026" />, and Stupa closed his tournament with the <ProductRef id="siux-electra-stupa-pro-st4-2025" />.

## The women's top four, in the same place once again

The only pair that truly suffered was **Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero (3)**: almost three hours against the Bordeaux P2 champions, Tamara Icardo —playing the <ProductRef id="starvie-triton-t-one-2027" />— and Claudia Jensen, trailing 1-3 and saving four break points in the third set before closing out 3-6, 6-4, 7-5. Ari held on with the <ProductRef id="adidas-arrow-hit-light-2026" /> and now awaits a repeat of the Milano final they won against **Paula Josemaría and Bea González (2)**, who came through against Rufo-Iglesias (6-2, 6-4); Bea plays the <ProductRef id="bullpadel-pearl-2027" />. In the top half, **Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea (1)** swept past Dal Pozzo-Rodríguez (6-1, 6-0) —Triay with the <ProductRef id="bullpadel-elite-02-2027" /> and Brea with the <ProductRef id="bullpadel-vertex-05-woman-2026" />— and meet **Claudia Fernández and Martina Calvo (4)**, who once again beat their former partners Ortega-Araújo (6-1, 6-0) and have reached the semi-finals at all eight tournaments they have played together; Claudia plays the <ProductRef id="bullpadel-wonder-2027" />. The stat that matters: Triay-Brea have won four of the six Majors they have contested since joining forces.
