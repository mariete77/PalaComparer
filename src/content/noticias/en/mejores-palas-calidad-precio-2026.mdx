## The golden rule

In padel, a paddle from two seasons ago isn't a worse paddle: it's the same material with a different paint job. Brands refresh every year and discounts punish previous generations even when the construction is virtually identical. If you don't need the latest finish, buying last season's generation at 50%+ off is almost always the smartest decision.
