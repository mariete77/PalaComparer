If the seeds' debut had made one thing clear, the **round of 16 of the Alpine Paris Major** confirmed it: at Roland-Garros there is no comfortable match. Thursday delivered the statement win of the tournament —**Edu Alonso and Aimar Goñi knocking out Coki Nieto and Mike Yanguas**, the Madrid P1 finalists, 6-4, 6-3— and set up a Spanish-flavoured quarter-final Friday that also marks the debut of **Red Bull TV** as the Major's broadcaster.

## Round of 16: Goñi-Alonso's upset and the illustrious casualties

The Spanish pair completed Thursday's big upset with a serious, no-nonsense performance, and they were not the only big-name victims. **Jesús Moya and Thomas Leygue**, the home hope, also fell to **Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz** (7-5, 6-3), and **Momo González and Lucas Campagnolo** had to come from behind against José Jiménez and Álex Arroyo (3-6, 6-1, 6-3) to earn the right to face Lebrón.

Full men's round-of-16 results:

- **Edu Alonso / Aimar Goñi** d. Mike Yanguas / Coki Nieto **6-4, 6-3**
- **Franco Stupaczuk / Jon Sanz** d. Jesús Moya / Thomas Leygue **7-5, 6-3**
- **Leo Augsburger / Juan Lebrón** d. Javi Barahona / Maxi Sánchez **4-6, 7-6(6), 6-4**
- **Momo González / Lucas Campagnolo** d. José Jiménez / Álex Arroyo **3-6, 6-1, 6-3**
- **Agustín Tapia / Arturo Coello** d. Álex Ruiz / Javi García **6-4, 6-3**
- **Ale Galán / Fede Chingotto** d. Álex Chozas / Juani De Pascual **6-3, 6-2**
- **Fran Guerrero / Javi Leal** d. Íñigo Jofre / Jairo Bautista **6-3, 6-4**
- **Martín Di Nenno / Paquito Navarro** d. Javi Martínez / Luis Hernández **6-3, 6-3**

In the women's draw the favourites took care of business: **Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea** (7-5, 6-3 over Rodríguez-Dal Pozzo), **Paula Josemaría and Bea González** (6-1, 6-0), **Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero** (6-1, 6-2) and **Martina Calvo and Claudia Fernández** (7-6(3), 6-1) are through to the quarter-finals. The day's notes came from **Carolina Orsi and Patty Llaguno**, into a quarter-final for the first time this season after an emphatic 6-1, 6-1, from **Ale Salazar and Aranza Osoro**, and from the emerging pair of **Marina Guinart and Alejandra Alonso**, who took out Sofía Saiz and Lucía Sainz 6-0, 6-2.

## Today's schedule: five duels with a Spanish accent

Friday spreads the quarter-finals between Philippe Chatrier and Suzanne-Lenglen, with a spectacular card:

- **13:00, Chatrier — Marta Ortega / Sofia Araújo vs Paula Josemaría / Bea González.** The Madrid finalists open the show court.
- **To follow, Chatrier — Tapia-Coello vs Guerrero-Leal.** The No. 1s, defending a third consecutive Paris crown, against the Spanish revelation pair.
- **Not before 18:00, Chatrier — Triay-Brea vs Orsi-Llaguno.** The defending champion No. 1s against the day's surprise guests.
- **Not before 20:00, Chatrier — Edu Alonso / Aimar Goñi vs Ale Galán / Fede Chingotto.** The slayers of Yanguas-Nieto against the Madrid champions: the ultimate test.
- **13:00, Lenglen — Ari Sánchez / Andrea Ustero vs Ale Salazar / Aranza Osoro.** Spanish padel at its purest in Salazar's farewell season.
- **To follow, Lenglen — Momo González / Lucas Campagnolo vs Augsburger-Lebrón.** Momo and his Wilson against Lebrón's comeback run.
- **Last match, Lenglen — Stupaczuk-Sanz vs Di Nenno-Paquito Navarro.** The clash of the day: three top-10 players on one court and the round's Argentina-Spain duel.

From today until Sunday's final, the tournament airs on **Red Bull TV** (worldwide, excluding the territories where other broadcasters hold rights), which starts its Major coverage precisely at the quarter-final stage.
