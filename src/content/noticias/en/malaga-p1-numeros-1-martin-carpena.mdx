The **Andalucía Málaga Premier Padel P1** wrapped up its week with both world No.1 pairs lifting the title, although through completely different paths. More than **44,000 spectators** passed through the Martín Carpena arena over the tournament.

## The women's final: three hours of war

**Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea** needed over three hours to overcome **Paula Josemaría and Bea González** in a final with five service breaks in the first set alone. The final score, 7-5, 3-6, 7-6(3), reflects the level of a final in which Josemaría and González served for the championship at 6-5 in the third.

<Callout title="The key stat">
Bea González, the local favourite, came within two points of defending her title in her home city. Triay and Brea came back and won the tie-break 7-3.
</Callout>

## The men's final: 53 minutes

In the men's draw, **Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia** gave no chance to **Juan Lebrón and Leo Augsburger**: 6-2, 6-2 in just 53 minutes. Lebrón and Augsburger arrived full of confidence after eliminating Federico Chingotto and Alejandro Galán in the semi-finals, but the No.1s controlled the match with accurate lobs that shut down their rivals' attacking game.

## A record week

The tournament confirmed padel's pull in Spain: 44,000 spectators at the Martín Carpena and a level of play that sets the bar high for the upcoming stops, with **Madrid P1** in late August and the **Paris Major** in September.
