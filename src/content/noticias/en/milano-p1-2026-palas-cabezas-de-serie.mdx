The **Oysho Milano Premier Padel P1** heads into its second day of round-of-32 action at the **Allianz Cloud** this Wednesday with the big names finally on court: the top four men's seeds all make their debut today. **Tapia-Coello (1)** open against qualifiers Ayats-Sánchez Chamero, **Galán-Chingotto (2)** meet the Portuguese Deus brothers, **Augsburger-Lebrón (3)** face Quílez-Mouriño and **Stupa-Sanz (4)** close the day against Solano-Pereyra. The women's draw also wraps up its first round today, with the top four favourites — Triay-Brea, Josemaría-Bea González, Sánchez-Ustero and Fernández-Calvo — spared the first round and debuting straight into Thursday's round of 16.

## Tuesday's scare

The first day of the round of 32 delivered the tournament's first big upset: **Jofre-Esbrí (12)** fell 6-3 6-3 to **Diestro-Sintes**, while the other seeds were made to work hard to varying degrees. **Di Nenno-Paquito Navarro (5)** gave Jensen-Gala no chance (6-3 6-3), **Yanguas-Lijó (8)** beat Ruiz-González (7-6(5) 6-2) and **Momo González-Campagnolo (7)** needed a comeback against Barahona-Piotto (4-6 6-3 6-2). Seeds 6, 11, 15 and 16 — Guerrero-Leal, Bautista-Bergamini, Libaak-Alfonso and Garrido-De Pascual — all wrapped it up in straight sets too. In the women's draw, **Salazar-Osoro (8)** posted the day's most commanding scoreline (6-0 6-3 over Collombon-Sharifova) and **Velasco-Eugenio** caused a stir by knocking out Guinart-Alonso (7), 4-6 6-4 7-5.

## Milan, the penultimate European stop of the year

The tournament — a fifth edition in Milan, €479,068 in prize money and **1,000 points** for the champions — lands right in the middle of the race for No. 1: **Tapia-Coello** (39,822 FIP ranking points) defend their status with 11 titles in 2026 and the Paris-Rotterdam-Düsseldorf European treble, while **Galán-Chingotto** (35,446) want to lift the trophy again at the Allianz Cloud, where they won last year. The top two seeds sit in opposite halves of the draw, so a rematch can only come in Sunday's final. Behind them, **Stupa-Sanz** enter their first event as fourth seeds after three finals without a trophy (Lyon, Rotterdam and Düsseldorf) — and they already know what it takes to beat the world's second-best pair this season.

## In the women's draw, the 2025 champions return separately

A year ago, **Ari Sánchez and Paula Josemaría** lifted the trophy together at the Allianz Cloud. In 2026 they return to the same stage with new partners: Ari with **Andrea Ustero** (the No. 3 seeds, who reached the 100th final of her career in Rotterdam aged just 29); Paula alongside **Bea González** (No. 2). Ahead of them, **Triay-Brea** arrive as the draw's top seeds, with the young **Fernández-Calvo** — champions in London and Düsseldorf — looming as the fourth pair. ## What's next at the Allianz Cloud

The round of 32 concludes this Wednesday (from 9:30, local time); the round of 16 follows on Thursday 15, the quarter-finals on Friday 16 (from 9:00) and the semi-finals on Saturday 17 (from 14:00) before Sunday's final (from 15:00). The tournament can be followed live on Premier Padel's channels — in Spain, via Disney+ — and Milan keeps raising the bar: the 2025 edition drew more than 35,000 fans, and Italian padel keeps booming — in Lombardy courts are up 180% since 2021 and the country is closing in on 1.5 million players, according to the organisers' own figures.
