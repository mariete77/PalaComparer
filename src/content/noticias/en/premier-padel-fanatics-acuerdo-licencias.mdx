Premier Padel announced a multi-year agreement with **Fanatics**, the global leader in licensed sports merchandise behind the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL and Formula 1, as **Master Licensee** of the tour. The deal covers e-commerce, in-venue retail, wholesale, product and memorabilia.

## Official store already live

As part of the agreement, the official store at **store.premierpadel.com** is already running, operated by Fanatics and integrated into its global e-commerce ecosystem. The launch collection brings together **Bullpadel, Homiés Marbella, Fanatics Branded and Wilson** across men's and women's ranges, capturing padel's jump from the court into urban culture.

## Next steps: London pop-up and Finals retail

In August, a **pop-up in London** will give fans an early in-person look at the collection, coinciding with the tour's debut in the British capital. In-venue retail arrives in December, with the **Qatar Airways Premier Padel Finals** hosting the first official store at a tournament venue.
