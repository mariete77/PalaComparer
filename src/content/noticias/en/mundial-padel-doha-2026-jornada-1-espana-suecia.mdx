Two ties, two 3-0s, zero sets dropped. Read off the scoresheet, Spain's opening day at the **Doha World Padel Championships** was a stroll. Seen from court 5 at the **Khalifa International Tennis and Squash Complex**, it had more to it: **Aimar Goñi** and **Mike Yanguas** needed to come back from a first-set tie-break against Sweden. Monday 2 November gave Spain a perfect record and a warning about the level of their game.

## The 3-0 that hid a scare

**Coello and Paquito** opened the tie with **6-3, 6-2** over Vásquez and Windahl. A comfortable scoreline for a pair still finding its rhythm: there were balls down the middle that nobody claimed, the kind that need a shout and a decision. The partnership has plenty of arguments — the world No 1 and one of the cleanest backhands on tour — but a World Cup is decided in five days and there is no room for long tune-ups.

The scare came next. **Axelsson and Släryd** took an endless first-set tie-break and forced **Yanguas and Goñi** to row upstream: **6-7, 7-5, 6-2**. The World Cup debut weighed on the 20-year-old from Navarre — his first senior World Championships — and he never quite freed his arm until the third set. Yanguas's reach at the net and a 6-2 closing set saved the point. The 3-0 came from the cleanest pair of the day: **Lebrón and Sanz, 6-0, 6-2** over Olsson and Frost.

In the other Group B tie, **Paraguay** swept the United Arab Emirates 3-0. They are Spain's opponents on Tuesday (16:00 CET, court 5) and, on paper, the toughest test of the group stage.

## Argentina give nothing away

The defending men's champions had no doubts at all. **Argentina 3-0 Australia without dropping a set**, including a double bagel: **Augsburger and Di Nenno** won 6-0, 6-0 against Zelba and Navarro. Earlier, **Stupaczuk and Chingotto** opened with 6-1, 6-1 over Bechard and Melisi, and **Alfonso and Arce** — the two new faces in the APA squad — closed it out 6-1, 6-0.

The story of the group came in the other tie: **the Netherlands beat France 2-1**, with two of the three matches going the distance. **Bart Van Opstal and Julian Prins** took the decider against Leygue and Blanqué, **6-4, 1-6, 6-3**. If the Dutch repeat that level, the quarter-final draw gets awkward for everyone. In Group C, **Germany** came from behind to beat **Chile 2-1** and **Portugal** swept Qatar 3-0; in Group D, **Italy** and **Brazil** both opened with 3-0 wins over the United States and Mexico.

## The champions show no mercy

**Carolina Navarro's** team defended their title without conceding an inch: **3-0 over Germany** while dropping only **two games across six sets**. **Claudia Fernández and Bea González** opened with 6-0, 6-1 over Kurz and Blaschke; **Paula Josemaría and Martina Calvo** repeated the script, 6-1, 6-0 against Renic and Höfer; and **Gemma Triay and Marta Ortega** closed with a **double 6-0** over Ermann and Schmitz. The pairing that raised the most questions when the squad was named produced the most severe scoreline of the day.

**Argentina** delivered too in Group B: 3-0 over Chile without a third set, through **Virginia Riera and Julieta Bidahorria** (6-0, 6-1), **Martina Fassio and Claudia Jensen** (6-2, 6-0) and a note for the future — **Victoria Vílchez, aged 16**, winning alongside **Aranzazu Osoro**, 6-2, 6-3. Elsewhere, **Brazil** brushed Great Britain aside (0-3), **Italy** did the same to Egypt (3-0), and **Sweden** were pushed harder than expected by Indonesia, who took the opening set in two of the three matches.

## Today, day 2: Spain v Great Britain and Spain v Paraguay

A double session for Spain. In the morning (**12:00 CET**, Grand Stand 2) the defending champions face **Great Britain**, beaten 0-3 by Brazil on day one. In the afternoon (**16:00**, court 5) Gutiérrez's men take on **Paraguay**, the tie that will most likely decide top spot in Group B. **Argentina v the Netherlands** (Grand Stand 2, 16:00) is the other match worth watching: the Dutch arrive with momentum after beating France.

In Spain the tournament is on **Movistar Plus+**; courts without broadcaster coverage are on the **FIP YouTube** channel and **Red Bull TV** in enabled territories. Quarter-finals on Thursday 5, semi-finals on Friday 6 and the finals on Saturday 7, with **€1.2 million in prize money** split equally between the two draws.
