The European indoor swing reaches its densest week this Monday: **two simultaneous ATP 500 events from October 26 to November 1**, the Erste Bank Open at Vienna's Wiener Stadthalle and the Swiss Indoors at Basel's St. Jakobshalle. Both arrive with A-list invitations: **Carlos Alcaraz accepted a wild card to return to Vienna for the first time since 2021**, and **Novak Djokovic did the same in Basel, where he hadn't played since 2011**. In between comes the definitive farewell of **Stan Wawrinka**, who closes his career at his home tournament.

## Vienna: Alcaraz returns with a stern entrance exam

The Spaniard, world No. 3 and fresh from a third-round Shanghai exit against Zizou Bergs, chases in Austria **his fourth title of the year** — and the draw offers no free passes: he opens against **Karen Khachanov**, a seven-time ATP Tour champion and semi-finalist at the recent US Open. History favors Alcaraz, who leads the head-to-head **6-0**. Past that test he would face Basilashvili or local wild card Rodionov, with American **Tommy Paul** as a potential quarter-final opponent. **Daniil Medvedev** also lurks in his half of the draw, sixth in the Race to Turin and hungry for points.

The other half is **Alexander Zverev** territory: the German top seed opens against #NextGenATP Martin Landaluce with an extra incentive — **if he reaches the quarter-finals he is guaranteed to rise to World No. 1 on November 2 for the first time in his career**, capping a year he has already locked up as year-end No. 1. Further down, a blockbuster opening round: **Tiafoe against Musetti**, with **Paul–Rublev** another match of Masters calibre.

## Basel: Djokovic hunts No. 103 and Wawrinka's last court

Djokovic returns to the tournament he **won on his 2009 debut** with two goals: career title **No. 103, which would tie Roger Federer's tally**, and points for the Race to Turin, where he sits 11th aiming at a record 19th qualification to the Nitto ATP Finals. His first opponent is an awkward one: **Ugo Humbert**, a lefty with four of his seven ATP titles won indoors who famously beat Alcaraz en route to the 2024 Rolex Paris Masters final. Top seed and former finalist **Ben Shelton** could wait in the semi-finals.

And then there is Wawrinka. At 41, after bidding farewell to the French crowd in Lyon last week — where he signed a 2h46 win over Landaluce before falling to Cobolli — the three-time Grand Slam champion and former World No. 3 **plays the final tournament of his career**. He draws tough **Jiri Lehecka** (8th seed) in the first round, an opponent he has never faced; survive that, and a qualifier follows, with Shelton a potential quarter-finalist opponent. A tribute alongside Federer, Monfils and Murray awaits him in Geneva in December.

The Swiss draw keeps loading up on names with a PalaComparer file: **Rafael Jodar**, ninth in the Race after [the Shanghai final](/noticias/shanghai-masters-2026-davidovich-fokina-campeon), debuts against Tabilo in their fourth meeting of the year; Shanghai champion **Alejandro Davidovich Fokina** opens against Bergs; and **Taylor Fritz** gets an instant rematch with Tsitsipas, who beat him in three sets in China two weeks ago and leads their head-to-head 5-2. Second seed Arthur Fils starts against Hurkacz.
