The **US Open 2026** is entering its second week in New York: the round of 16 takes place on **Sunday 6 and Monday 7 September**, with the quarter-finals on **Tuesday 8 and Wednesday 9** at Arthur Ashe Stadium. With **Jannik Sinner** absent, **Alexander Zverev** is the No.1 seed in the men's draw and **Carlos Alcaraz**, the defending champion, is No.2. On the women's side, **Aryna Sabalenka** is chasing her third consecutive title in Flushing Meadows. Here's a look at the rackets the favourites are carrying into the decisive phase — and which models you can find in our catalog.

## Alcaraz, into the fourth round with his Pure Aero 98

The Spaniard is already into week two: he beat **Yibing Wu** 6-3, 6-4, 6-1 and has now won **ten straight matches** at the tournament, where he defends his 2025 title. His racket remains the **Babolat Pure Aero 98**: 98 sq in, a **16x20** pattern and around 323 grams strung — the evolution of the Aero family (trimmed to 98 for extra control) that Babolat uses to build his heavy spin game. It's the catalog model also played by **Holger Rune**, and with it Alcaraz is trying to make Flushing Meadows his Grand Slam.

According to the draw, his natural quarter-final path would cross **Ben Shelton**, who faces Denis Shapovalov this Saturday for a spot in the fourth round.

## Zverev, the No.1 seed with the Head Speed MP

With Sinner out, the German arrives as **top seed** and is still alive after a marathon: he beat **Quentin Halys** 6-4, 4-6, 7-6(3), 6-7(3), 6-3 on Thursday. He plays with the **Head Speed MP** — 100 sq in, **16x19** and 315 grams — the all-rounder of the Speed family, the same line **Novak Djokovic** builds his baseline game on at age 39. The Speed MP is the catalog pick for anyone after the versatility of the model leading the tour.

The draw reserves a possible **Djokovic-Tiafoe** quarter-final in this section if both keep advancing.

## Women's draw: Blade, EZONE, Boom and VCORE lead in New York

- **Aryna Sabalenka** (seed 1) is already through to week two after beating Kamilla Rakhimova 6-3, 6-4. The Belarusian plays the **Wilson Blade 98 in its 18x20 version**, a pure control frame with which she seeks her **third straight US Open**; in the catalog you'll find the Blade 98 v10 16x19, the same family played by **Stefanos Tsitsipas**.
- **Jessica Pegula** (seed 3) came from behind to beat Leylah Fernandez (1-6, 6-4, 6-3) with her **Yonex EZONE 98**: 98 sq in, 16x19 and 320 grams — the comfortable, easy-power racket she shares with **Ben Shelton**, who carries it as his official factory kit.
- **Coco Gauff** (seed 4), fresh off winning Cincinnati, keeps playing with her **Head Boom MP** — 100 sq in, 16x19 and 312 grams — Head's line for players who want power with a margin for error.
- **Elena Rybakina** (seed 2) is playing the tournament with the **Yonex VCORE**, available in the catalog in both **VCORE 98** and **VCORE 100** versions — 321 and 300 grams strung respectively. The draw lines up a possible quarter-final against **Iga Swiatek**.

## The surprise of the week

The first week had its share of big-name exits: **Karen Khachanov** knocked out No.3 seed Félix Auger-Aliassime, and Botic van de Zandschulp did the same to Alex de Miñaur. The draw has opened up in a section nobody expected — and in tennis, the racket is only half the story.
