The **China Open in Beijing** (ATP 500) closes TODAY with a final few could have scripted: **Novak Djokovic (6) against Alex de Minaur (5)**. Both of Monday's semi-finals ended early — one by default, one by injury — and the tournament reaches its last day with everything on the line: the Serb's historic 33-0 in the Chinese capital, a seventh Beijing crown and a ticket in the race to the ATP Finals in Turin.

## Medvedev defaulted: "It was a shock"

The most anticipated match of Monday broke down in the cruellest way. With **Djokovic leading 7-5, 5-3** — three points from victory — a ball rebounded from the back of the court towards Medvedev, who instinctively hit it into the stands. The ball **struck a spectator in the face**, who had to leave his seat assisted by tournament staff.

The Russian was defaulted and Djokovic advanced to the final after two hours and 29 minutes of battle: he had come from a break down in the first set and saved multiple break points. "I was three points from winning. It was an exhausting battle... I don't know how many break points I saved, it was crazy," the Serb said. He was shaken by how it ended: "It was a shock, to be honest. I'm sorry for the person, I hope it's nothing serious. I also feel sorry for Daniil, because I know how it feels to be defaulted in a big match." Djokovic himself was defaulted at the 2020 US Open under similar circumstances.

Medvedev, who had arrived on a run after winning the Hangzhou title, later apologised: "I feel terribly sorry. The ball was bouncing back and I just tried to hit it anywhere so it wouldn't leave the court. When I saw the result, I felt horrible."

With the win, Djokovic is now **33 for 33 in Beijing** — nobody has ever beaten him there — and will play his **146th tour-level final**, one win away from his **102nd title** and his seventh in Beijing, a tournament he had not played since 2015.

## De Minaur reaches his first Beijing final

The second semi-final also ran short of its script, though without controversy: **Hurkacz retired with an adductor injury** while De Minaur led 6-4, 3-2 (40-0). The Pole, who had already felt discomfort in his quarter-final against Khachanov, could barely move in the second set. "I felt my adductor yesterday and I couldn't recover completely for today. I was struggling from the start," he explained.

The Australian, world No. 9 and his country's top-ranked player, broke Hurkacz's serve twice to lead 4-0 and withstood the comeback to take the opening set. It will be his **first China Open final** — and he is chasing his **second title of the season**, after Rotterdam in February.

## What's on the line

The final starts **not before 7 p.m. local time (1 p.m. CEST)** and is followed by the doubles final. The numbers:

- **Djokovic**: a seventh Beijing title, his first of 2026 and the 102nd of his career. Win, and he signs off a flawless **34-0** at the event. He leads the head-to-head 3-1 — their last meeting, at Wimbledon 2025, went his way — and this will be the first time they meet in a final.
- **De Minaur**: the chance to become the first player ever to beat Djokovic in Beijing, plus a second trophy of the year.

Also on the line: the **PIF ATP Live Race to Turin**. The winner leaves Beijing **ninth** — Djokovic climbs from 10th with a win — while eighth-placed Medvedev loses the 200 points he had earned before his default. Two Masters 1000 events remain (Shanghai and Paris) and Sinner's uncertain availability keeps the race open.
