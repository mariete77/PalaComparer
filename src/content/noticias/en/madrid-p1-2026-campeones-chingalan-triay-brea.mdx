The **Comunidad de Madrid Premier Padel P1 by Oysho** closed out on **Sunday, September 6** at the **Movistar Arena** with a double crown for the season's most in-form pairs: **Alejandro Galán and Fede Chingotto** took the men's draw and **Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea** the women's. Both finals went to three sets, and both leave the Race tighter than ever one week before the **Alpine Paris Major**.

## Chingalán: an epic final and a seventh title of the year

Galán and Chingotto beat **Coki Nieto and Mike Yanguas** **6-4, 5-7, 6-4** after more than two and a half hours on court. Nobody had made it this hard for them all season: Nieto-Yanguas had just knocked out the No.1s **Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia** in the quarter-finals (7-5, 7-6(5)) — the first time the top-ranked pair missed a semi-final since May 2025. Even that feat wasn't enough against a Chingalán who keep adding to their **seventh title of the year**.

The win carries a double prize: **Galán-Chingotto close in on the No.1 spot**. Coello-Tapia still lead the Race, but the gap has shrunk right before the tournament that hands out the most points of the year.

## Triay-Brea: a comeback to keep on winning

In the women's draw, **Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea** came from behind against **Paula Josemaría and Bea González**, who took the opening set in the tie-break, and ended up winning **6(5)-7, 6-3, 6-3**. The runners-up — who had turned a 0-5 third-set deficit into a 7-5 win against Claudia Fernández and Martina Calvo in the quarter-finals — could not repeat the feat in the final: Triay-Brea keep adding titles and extending their dominance on the women's circuit.

## The rackets behind the double

**Ale Galán** plays with the **adidas Metalbone 2026**, his official racket: a high-balance diamond with an **aluminised 16K carbon** face and the **Weight & Balance** system, which lets you add up to 11 grams and tune the balance for each match. Its more demanding version, the **Metalbone HRD+**, is also in the catalog.

**Fede Chingotto** completes the pair with the **Bullpadel Neuron 02 2026**: a medium-balance hybrid with **X-Tend Carbon 3K** faces and fiberglass layers, a firm, dry and highly precise touch with a **3D Grain** finish to control spin — the weapon behind his backhand game.

Both women's finalists play **Bullpadel**: **Gemma Triay** with the **Elite W 2026** (not yet in our catalog) and **Delfi Brea** with the **Vertex 05 Woman 2026**, the women's version of the Vertex 05 diamond mold: lighter (350-360 g), medium-high balance, **Fibrix** faces and a rough **Top Spin** finish — fully listed in the catalog. **Bea González**, meanwhile, plays the **Bullpadel Pearl**.
