If Tuesday belonged to [Bidahorria and Riera](/en/noticias/kuwait-major-2026-bidahorria-riera-favoritas-octavos), Wednesday's round of 16 at the **Kuwait Major** belonged to two young Argentines. **Tino Libaak and Gonzalo Alfonso** came back on **Juan Lebrón and Leo Augsburger (3)**, winning 4-6, 7-5, 6-1 on centre court — the second time this season they knock out the pair, having already beaten them in the Madrid P1 round of 16 (7-6, 7-5). The wall's name is Libaak. This Thursday (from 12:00 in Spain, on Red Bull TV) the quarter-finals decide the semi-finalists.

## The wall Lebrón keeps bouncing off

The world No. 30 pair is no anomaly any more: this season they have taken out Lebrón-Augsburger in two events — the Madrid P1 and Kuwait — and here they did it coming from a set down, with a 6-1 closing act. ## Sintes and Zamora, from qualifying to history

**Marc Sintes and Coquito Zamora** are no longer [Sunday's surprise](/en/noticias/kuwait-major-2026-sintes-zamora-sorpresa): they are quarter-finalists. On Wednesday they swept Javi García and Álex Ruiz 6-3, 7-5 to open their Major quarter-final account — a week ago they had never been past a round of 32 at this level. Juan Tello, <ProductRef id="bullpadel-vertex-05-2026" /> in hand, is the favourite on paper… but on paper so were Momo-Campagnolo and García-Ruiz.
