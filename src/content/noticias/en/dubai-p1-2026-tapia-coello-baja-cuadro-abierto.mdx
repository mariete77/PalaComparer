The **Dubai P1** was supposed to be one last chance to watch the world number ones close the season with another title. It won't be. **Agustín Tapia and Arturo Coello have withdrawn before playing their first match** because of the Argentine's injury, and the men's draw loses its top two pairs: theirs and **Galán and Chingotto's**, who were already out since last week.

The news broke on Tuesday 10 November, with the main draw already under way, and the pair announced it **on the recommendation of their medical team**. The statement gives no detail on the injury and no timeline, so the question now hangs over the final stretch of the season: the **Mexico Major (23-29 November)** and the **Barcelona Finals (10-13 December)**.

## The end of Dubai's only champions

Coello and Tapia were the only champions this tournament had ever had. They won in **2024** (6-4 6-3) and **2025** (6-3 6-4), both times beating Galán and Chingotto in the final. They arrived with the **year-end number one already sealed for a fourth straight season**, with **13 titles in 2026** and **five in a row** — Paris, Rotterdam, Düsseldorf, Milan and Kuwait — and with a shot at a third consecutive crown at the Hamdan Sports Complex. That chapter will have to wait.

Their place in the draw goes to a **lucky loser pair** from qualifying, who debut on Wednesday against **Álex Chozas and Juan Ignacio Rubini**, not before 16:00 Spanish time. It is the strangest slot in the tournament: whoever steps in finds a road to the round of 16 that was unthinkable 48 hours ago.

The other absence predates this one. **Ale Galán** is still carrying the hand injury that kept him out of Kuwait and the Doha World Cup, and **Federico Chingotto** chose not to play with a partner from the waiting list. Those two withdrawals have completely redrawn the top of the draw.

## A Dubai with no safety net

With the top two pairs gone, the bill changes hands and the tournament will have a **first-time champion** for the first time in its three editions.

- **Juan Lebrón and Leo Augsburger** become the highest-ranked pair in the draw. They are the second seeds and arrive straight from playing the World Cup final on opposite sides: Lebrón won the opening point for Spain, Augsburger lost his for Argentina.
- **Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz** (3) are the reference in the top half. Stupa is the only one of the eight players in the top four pairs who did not play the Doha final.
- **Martín Di Nenno and Paquito Navarro** (4) move up a step and avoid the number ones until a potential final.
- **Tino Libaak and Gonza Alfonso** are the big winners: the round-of-16 wall that was Coello-Tapia has vanished and their path to the quarter-finals has suddenly opened up. First they must get past **Tolito Aguirre and Pablo Lijó**.
- **Javi Leal and Fran Guerrero** got in by the back door — they missed the entry deadline, were given a qualifying wildcard and ended up in the main draw after Galán-Chingotto withdrew — and are listed as sixth seeds.

## Tuesday: Rufo and Iglesias land the first blow

The opening day produced the first big shock in the women's draw: **Lorena Rufo and Victoria Iglesias knocked out Alejandra Salazar and Aranza Osoro**, the eighth seeds, **6-3 2-6 6-2**. It is the most painful defeat of the day given the stage of the season, with the Barcelona places still up for grabs.

The men's round of 64 was completed. **Marc Sintes and Coquito Zamora** backed up the form they showed at the Kuwait Major — where they reached their first round of 16 at a Major — and now await **Coki Nieto and Mike Yanguas**. **Chozas and Rubini** beat Pincho Fernández and Cepero **6-2 6-4** and are the pair waiting for the lucky loser replacing the world number ones.

## Wednesday: 22 matches and the debuts

The second day of the main draw starts at **12:00 in Dubai (09:00 in Spain)** with **22 matches** across four courts: 16 men's round-of-32 ties and 6 women's.

The debuts that matter most:

- **Di Nenno and Paquito** open centre court against **Aragón and Roglán**.
- **Lebrón and Augsburger** face **Castaño and Pol Hernández**.
- **Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz** close centre court against **Ayats and Emilio Sánchez**.
- **Coki Nieto and Yanguas** meet **Sintes and Zamora**, the tie of the day.
- **Libaak and Alfonso** take on **Aguirre and Lijó**.
- **Guerrero and Leal** face **Calleja and Romera**.
- **Tello and Arce** play **Domínguez and Sager**.

In the women's draw, **Claudia Jensen and Tamara Icardo** and **Marta Ortega and Sofía Araújo** enter, while the top four seeds wait for Thursday's round of 16.

## What is at stake

The Dubai P1 pays **€479,068** and **1,000 points** to the winning pair: **€26,000 per player** in the men's draw and **€17,000** in the women's. It is the **last P1 of the season** — the Mexico Major and the Barcelona Finals follow — and with the top two pairs out, every round is worth double for anyone still fighting for a place at the Palau Sant Jordi.
