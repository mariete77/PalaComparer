The **Rolex Shanghai Masters** (7-18 October) produced its biggest surprise of the week on Friday: **Hubert Hurkacz knocked out Novak Djokovic** (6-4, 6-3) to claim his **first win in nine meetings** against the Serb.

## Hurkacz's first

The Pole, champion here in 2023, needed just **87 minutes** and **13 aces** to take down the 10th seed on his return to the court where he lifted his only Masters 1000 title. He saved both break points he faced, and a **Djokovic double fault in the fourth game of the second set** (3-1) put the match beyond doubt. The head-to-head, which stood at **0-8**, now reads 1-8.

Djokovic arrived in Shanghai just three days after lifting his **102nd title** in Beijing, with **six matches in ten days** behind him, and admitted the toll: «I was not close to what I was playing in Beijing. I'm slightly more tired, not fresh… It's a super quick surface, completely different from Beijing. If you lose your serve, it is enough to lose the match, once per set, and that is what happened. It is very difficult to return his serve, almost impossible». The Serb remains the tournament's most decorated player —**four titles, a record**— but his 2026 China campaign ends in the second round. Hurkacz, world No. 34, awaits his third-round opponent.

<Callout title="The stat">
Hurkacz needed nine attempts: against Djokovic he had lost eight in a row, most recently at the 2024 US Open. On Friday, with 13 aces and without dropping serve, he ended the streak in 87 minutes.
</Callout>

## Zverev and Shelton advance

Top seed **Alexander Zverev** had to work harder than expected against home wild card **Yibing Wu** (6-4, 7-6(4)) but remains firmly on track in his bid for the **ATP World No. 1 ranking**, with his Turin spot already secured. **Ben Shelton** (3) also moved on: **6-3, 7-6(6)** against Daniel Altmaier, Rune's conqueror, on his **24th birthday**.
