**Alexander Zverev** has won the **2026 US Open**, defeating **Ben Shelton** **6-3, 7-6(2), 5-7, 6-2** on Sunday at Arthur Ashe Stadium. It is his **second Grand Slam title — and second of the year** after Roland Garros in June, the definitive confirmation of a resurgence that started in Paris. For Shelton, who was aiming to become the first American man to win a major since Andy Roddick in 2003, the final was another step in a historic fortnight: his Tuesday win over Alcaraz in the latest-finishing match in tournament history is already etched in stone.

## The match: the top seed's cold efficiency in his sixth final

Zverev walked into the final with every number on his side: a **5-0 head-to-head** against Shelton (who had taken just one set off him ever), **44-1 against left-handers since the 2023 French Open**, and the experience of **six Grand Slam finals** — his third this year alone — against an opponent playing the first of his career. And he played like someone who knows history weighs: a huge serve, the backhand crosscourt to the corner, and the patience to give away nothing in the big moments.

Shelton, carried by a devoted Ashe crowd, hung on through the second-set surge and stole the third when the German wobbled on serve for the first time. But the fourth was a monologue: Zverev broke early, put everything back in play, and closed out the title with the authority of a man who had been here before. (Sources: USOpen.org, The Guardian.)

## Gravity Tour vs EZONE 98: two ways to read 98 square inches

Both are 98-square-inch, 16x19 frames for advanced players — and they still play opposite brands of tennis. The final's duel, in specs:

- **Weight and balance**: the Gravity Tour Zverev at 323 g strung (4 pts head-light, ~318 mm); the EZONE 98 at 320 g (~320 mm). Nearly identical on the scale, but the Head carries the higher swingweight (326) while the Yonex prioritizes maneuverability.
- **Stiffness**: **RA 61 for the Gravity vs RA 63 for the EZONE**. The Head is more elastic and comfortable; the Yonex returns a bit more free energy at contact.
- **Philosophy**: the Gravity Tour is control with spin access — for whoever builds the point with depth and finishes flat; the EZONE is instant power and a generous sweet spot — for whoever lives on the first strike. Zverev vs Shelton, distilled into two frames.
