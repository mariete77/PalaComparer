The **main draw of the FIP Platinum Lyon 2026 gets underway today, Wednesday September 23**, at the Palais des Sports de Gerland, with the padel world back in France. It is the **fourth of the five FIP Platinum events of the season** and puts **300 Race points toward the Qatar Airways Premier Padel Finals in Barcelona** — the December showdown at the Palau Sant Jordi — on the line: after Lyon, only the Platinum in **Aguascalientes (Mexico)**, the week before the Acapulco Major, remains to add points. (Sources: padelfip.com, studypadel.com, tennis-tonic, padel-magazine.it.)

## An indoor event, €130,000 on offer and an open title race

Lyon brings together **28 pairs in the men's draw** (22 direct entries, 4 qualifiers and 2 wildcards) on indoor courts split between the Palais des Sports de Gerland and the All In Country Academy, with **€130,000 in prize money** — €8,125 per player for the champions. The 2025 edition was won by **Alex Ruiz and Juanlu Esbri** in the men's event and **Alejandra Salazar and Martina Calvo** in the women's, and **no pair has repeated as champion** across the editions played: this year will crown new winners again.

## Stupaczuk-Sanz, top seeds with both rackets in the catalog

**Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz** arrive as the No. 1 men's pair. Stupa plays with the **Siux Electra Pro IT 26** — <ProductRef id="siux-electra-pro-it-26-2026" /> — and already knows what it takes to win a Platinum this season: he did it in **Tirana alongside Mike Yanguas**, adding the FIP Gold in Belgrade with Sanz. His partner plays the **Drop Shot Axion Attack 2.0 2026** — <ProductRef id="drop-shot-axion-attack-2-0-2026" /> — and both his [player page](/jugadores/franco-stupaczuk) and [Jon Sanz's](/jugadores/jon-sanz) are already in the catalog.

The other three seeds, with byes straight into the round of 16, are **Javi Leal and Fran Guerrero (No. 2)**, **Martin Di Nenno and Juan Tello (No. 3)** and **Momo Gonzalez and Lucas Campagnolo (No. 4)**. Two of those rackets have entries on PalaComparer: **Tello's Bullpadel Vertex 05 2026** — <ProductRef id="bullpadel-vertex-05-2026" /> — (the Argentine has his full family in the catalog, including the Vertex 04 and the Indiga Power) and **Momo's Wilson Endure Pro V1** — <ProductRef id="wilson-endure-pro-v1-2026" /> — with his [player page](/jugadores/momo-gonzalez).

## Salazar, defending her title in her final year as a pro

In the women's draw, **Alejandra Salazar and Aranzazu Osoro** are the No. 1 seeds. Salazar, 40, in her **last season as a professional**, defends the title she won in Lyon in 2025 alongside a 17-year-old Martina Calvo; she now shares the court with Osoro, her partner at the Madrid P1. Her racket in the catalog is the **Bullpadel Flow Woman** — <ProductRef id="bullpadel-flow-2024" /> — and her final big goal is clear: **to qualify for the Barcelona Finals and close a record-breaking career at the Palau Sant Jordi** — 58 titles and the most decorated player in history. Behind them, **Ale Alonso with Giulia Dal Pozzo** (a new pairing, No. 2), **Carmen Goenaga and Bea Caldera** (No. 3) and **Lorena Rufo-Jessica Castello** (No. 4, runners-up at the FIP Gold Sao Paulo a few weeks ago).

## Today's first round, with Spanish and French flavour

The opening day features a clash where both rackets on court are in the catalog: **Javier Garrido and Sanyo Gutierrez** — **Wilson Defy Pro V1** (<ProductRef id="wilson-defy-pro-v1-2026" />) and **StarVie Eternal 2027** (<ProductRef id="starvie-eternal-2027" />), with [Sanyo's player page](/jugadores/sanyo-gutierrez) — face the home pair **Adam Axelsson-Dylan Guichard** in the afternoon. **Juanlu Esbri**, last year's champion, also plays today, now alongside Inigo Jofre. The French presence is strong: **Alix Collombon** with Ksenia Sharifova, **Lea Godallier** with Daiara Valenzuela and **Louise Bahurel** with Ivet Val Lopez, plus wildcards Steffi Merah-Charlotte Soubrie and Marie-Amelie Dardaine-Clara Mansart; in the men's draw, **Timeo Fonteny** — a Paris Major round-of-16 player with Jesus Moya — teams up with Nacho Moragues.
