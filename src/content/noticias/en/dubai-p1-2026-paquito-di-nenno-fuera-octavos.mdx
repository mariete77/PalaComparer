The men's draw at the **Dubai P1** had a clear hierarchy once Coello-Tapia and Galán-Chingotto were gone: Lebrón-Augsburger on one side, Stupaczuk-Sanz on the other, and **Paquito Navarro and Martín Di Nenno** as the fourth seeds with a clean run to the final. That order lasted one match.

**Di Nenno and Paquito are out.** They lost their opener to **Manuel Aragón Herrera and Albert Roglán Pons** — a pair that came through qualifying and sits at **No. 93 and No. 88** in the world — **6-0 4-6 2-6**. The first set was a Spanish monologue; the errors arrived in the second and the third slipped away without an argument. It is the shock of the tournament and leaves the draw with **two of its top four seeds gone**: them, and Coello-Tapia, who withdrew before playing.

## The 6-0 that hid everything

The first-set scoreline is misleading. Aragón and Roglán did not stumble into the main draw: they won three qualifying matches to get there and then ran into a seeded pair that handed them the opening set. From 6-0 onwards, the match changed hands.

Paquito and Di Nenno, fourth seeds because of the Galán-Chingotto withdrawal, leave without winning a set beyond the first — and with the feeling of having let the best chance of their season slip.

## Lebrón and Augsburger, on the edge

The new favourites had a scare of their own. **Juan Lebrón and Leo Augsburger** dropped the first set and were a break down in the second against **Manu Castaño and Pol Hernández**, and only then did the Lebrón from the Doha World Cup final show up: more chiquitas, sharper angles, the match dragged back to his terms. **4-6 6-3 6-3** after an hour and a half of fighting.

In the round of 16 they meet **Víctor Ruiz and Facundo López**, who came back against **Barahona and Piotto** (6-4 3-6 4-6), the 16th seeds.

## Stupaczuk and Sanz spent nothing

The third seeds had the smoothest start of anyone. **Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz** were 6-3 and 5-1 up when **Emilio Sánchez Chamero** retired injured alongside Arnau Ayats: **55 minutes** and a place in the last 16 with no mileage on the clock.

Next up: **Maxi Sánchez and Sanyo Gutiérrez**, who got through with more trouble than expected against Goenaga and Collado. It is the generational clash of the round — two former world No. 1s against the pair that has reached three straight finals on tour, all lost to Coello-Tapia.

## The rest of Wednesday

- **Libaak and Alfonso**, 6-3 6-4 over **Aguirre and Lijó**. The world championship runners-up look sharp and now face lucky losers **Marcos González and Diego García**, who pulled off the upset of the first round by beating Rubini and Chozas (6-4 3-6 7-6).
- **Tello and Arce**, 6-4 6-4 over **Domínguez and Sager**. Next: **Edu Alonso and Aimar Goñi** (6-3 6-4 over Zapata and Naranjo).
- **Yanguas and Coki Nieto** had a real fight: **6-7 6-3 6-4** against **Sintes and Zamora**, who arrived from the Kuwait Major quarter-finals. Next: **De Pascual and Garrido** (6-3 7-6 over Valenzuela and Gil).
- **Momo González and Campagnolo**, 6-3 7-5 over José Luis González and Javier Ruiz. They meet **Jofre and Esbrí** (6-4 7-6 over Javi Martínez and Luis Hernández).
- **Guerrero and Leal**, 6-3 6-4 over Calleja and Romera. Against **Bautista and Bergamini**, who needed three sets against Mouriño and Quílez (7-5 2-6 6-3).
- Another notable exit: **Álex Ruiz and Javi García**, the 13th seeds, lost to **Abbate and Dal Bianco** 4-6 6-1 6-4. The Italian-Argentine pair face **Aragón and Roglán**, the men who sent Paquito and Di Nenno home.

With Paquito and Di Nenno out, the Dubai P1 will crown a brand-new champion for the first time in its three editions, and the only pair that had ever lifted the trophy is not in the draw.

## Women: a first match point for No. 1

The women's top four debut in the round of 16 on Thursday with more than a quarter-final place at stake. **Delfi Brea and Gemma Triay** arrive at the Hamdan Sports Complex with a **2,780-point lead** over **Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero** in the race for world No. 1 and, depending on results, this is their **first match point** to finish the year on top for the second season running.

They open against **Marta Talaván and Sofía Saiz**, a pair that already had a scare in the first round. The other three seeds also enter:

- **Josemaría and Bea González** (2) against **Fassio and Virseda**. Bea is the only player with two Dubai titles — one with Brea, one with Claudia Fernández.
- **Ari Sánchez and Ustero** (3) against **Manquillo and Lucía Sainz**.
- **Claudia Fernández and Martina Calvo** (4) against **Castelló and Rodríguez**.

The upset of the women's draw came from **Cánovas and Lobo**, who blew past **Caldera and Goenaga** (6-3 6-0) and now face **Guinart and Ale Alonso**.
