The ninth Laver Cup will be decided **on Sunday at The O2 in London** after a breathless Saturday left the score at **7-5 for Europe**. The final day hands out **three points per win** and the first team to 13 takes the trophy: **two wins** would do for Europe, while Team World need at least **three**. Play opens at **12:00 in London (1:00 PM in Spain) with a doubles**, match number 9, followed by three singles and an extra doubles only in the event of a 12-12 tie after the twelfth match. The line-ups were made public after Saturday's close (sources: lavercup.com, atptour.com, Laver Cup official channels).

## Tien debuts in "lockdown mode": 6-3, 2-6, 10-7 over Cobolli

Debutant **Learner Tien** opened the day by coming from behind to beat Flavio Cobolli in the Laver Breaker (10-7) in what the organization described as "lockdown mode" — and, in passing, **levelled the overall score at 3-3** minutes before things turned sour for Europe. The Italian, who plays with the **HEAD Radical Pro family** — its 2026 generation is in our catalog: <ProductRef id="head-radical-pro-2026" /> —, had stretches of control but couldn't close it out.

## The comeback of the tournament: De Minaur saves a match point and downs Zverev

**Alex de Minaur** produced the feat of Saturday: **2-6, 7-6(5), 11-9** over US Open champion **Alexander Zverev**, saving a match point in the Laver Breaker — the ATP Tour itself called it an "extraordinary comeback". With that win, Team World led **5-3 for the first time in the event**. The Australian plays a **Wilson Ultra Pro 99**, a frame with no retail version — which is why you won't find it in our catalog.

## Alcaraz answers back: saves match points and exacts his revenge (13-11)

**Carlos Alcaraz** paid back the punch Fritz landed in San Francisco 2025 (6-3, 6-2 during Team World's sweep): with the <ProductRef id="babolat-pure-aero-98-2026" />, the Spaniard **saved match points** and settled the Laver Breaker **13-11** — the official recap was titled "Alcaraz exacts revenge in O2 thriller" — leaving Saturday at 2-2 in matches and the overall at 5-5. Fritz, with his <ProductRef id="head-radical-mp-auxetic" />, was within a point of a second straight win over the Spaniard.

## Ruud & Zverev seal the doubles: 6-4, 7-6(9)

The night doubles gave the lead back to Europe: **Casper Ruud and Alexander Zverev** beat **Bublik and Nakashima** 6-4, 7-6(9), with the German redeeming himself hours after his singles defeat and playing with the <ProductRef id="head-gravity-tour-zverev-2026" /> he used to lift the US Open. Alcaraz and Ruud, both **2-0**, are the only undefeated players of the edition; Nakashima, with his <ProductRef id="yonex-vcore-98-2026" />, and Menšík, with the <ProductRef id="wilson-blade-98-v10-2026" />, look set to be key pieces of the finale, along with Jódar — <ProductRef id="head-speed-mp-2026" /> — and Ruud, who plays the EZONE 100 and whose 98 sq-in sister, the <ProductRef id="yonex-ezone-98-2025" />, is in our catalog. On the other side, Team World chase something unprecedented: their only titles (London 2022 and San Francisco 2025) have never been back-to-back.
