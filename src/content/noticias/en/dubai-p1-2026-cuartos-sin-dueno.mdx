The Dubai P1 reaches the quarter-finals with the draw open since day one: no Tapia and Coello, no Galán and Chingotto, and no clear favourite for Sunday. Friday 13 November is the first day broadcast in full on **Red Bull TV** — until now the matches went out on Premier Padel's YouTube channel — and one number sums up the tournament: **of the eight men's pairs still alive, only one has won a Premier Padel event this season**. That is **Juan Lebrón and Leo Augsburger**, at the Brussels P2 in April, coming from behind to beat Coello and Tapia in the final (2-6, 6-3, 6-3).

Every other pair left —Stupaczuk-Sanz, Libaak-Alfonso, Tello-Arce, Momo-Campagnolo, Bautista-Bergamini, De Pascual-Garrido and Abbate-Dal Bianco— is chasing a first title on the tour. And at a P1 worth €479,068 and 1,000 points, with the two best pairs in the ranking watching from home, that door does not open again this season.

## What the round of 16 left behind

Thursday was the busiest day of the tournament and it took out two seeds:

- **De Pascual and Garrido knocked out Yanguas and Coki Nieto (5)** 7-6, 3-6, 7-6 in 2h34. The seeds took both tie-breaks and still lost the match on the details.
- **Bautista and Bergamini came back against Leal and Guerrero** (1-6, 7-5, 6-3 in 1h35), a duel between two pairs arriving hungry after the World Cup.
- **Libaak and Alfonso** gave Marcos González and Diego García nothing: 6-2, 6-3.
- **Stupaczuk and Sanz** beat Sanyo Gutiérrez and Maxi Sánchez 6-3, 6-4 and have still not dropped a set in the event.
- **Momo González and Campagnolo** were just as clean: 6-3, 6-3 against Jofre and Esbrí.
- **Arce and Tello** lost the first set to Edu Alonso and Aimar Goñi and turned it around (3-6, 6-3, 6-2).
- **Abbate and Dal Bianco** ended the run of **Aragón and Roglán**, the qualifiers who had knocked out Paquito and Di Nenno: 7-6, 6-3.
- **Lebrón and Augsburger** closed the draw by beating Víctor Ruiz and Facundo López.

In the women's draw the top four came through: **Triay and Brea** won 6-3, 6-1 against Talaván and Saiz; **Fernández and Calvo** had to work harder than expected against Castelló and Rodríguez (7-6, 6-4); **Josemaría and Bea** and **Ari Sánchez and Ustero** are also through. The surprise of the round came from **Rufo and Iglesias**, who came back against Amanda López and Camila Fassio (6-4, 4-6, 6-2).

## The four men's quarter-finals

**Momo González-Campagnolo v Abbate-Dal Bianco** (around 14:00, Court 1). The Italian-Argentine pair are the lowest-ranked left — Abbate is world No. 53, Dal Bianco No. 80 — and they have not dropped a set. Momo and Campagnolo have not dropped one either. It is the most accessible quarter of the draw and, at the same time, the trickiest: nobody expected Abbate and Dal Bianco to be here.

**Lebrón-Augsburger (2) v Arce-Tello** (around 17:30, centre court). The tastiest tie. Tello and Arce have just played the Kuwait Major final, which they lost 6-1, 6-1 to Coello and Tapia, and in Dubai they have grown through the week. Lebrón and Augsburger, who already had a scare in their opener (4-6, 6-3, 6-3 against Castaño and Pol Hernández), are the only pair in the draw with a Premier Padel title in 2026 and the ones under the most pressure: if they do not win here, the year ends without another P1.

**Libaak-Alfonso v Bautista-Bergamini** (not before 19:00, centre court). Libaak and Alfonso have been the sharpest pair in the event: two wins without losing a set and the rhythm that took them to the Kuwait Major semi-finals. Bautista and Bergamini arrive on the high of coming back from 1-6 down.

**Stupaczuk-Sanz (3) v De Pascual-Garrido** (around 20:30, centre court). The prize is big: Stupa and Sanz are the only top-three seeds left, they have won both matches without dropping a set and they arrive with two P2 finals this year (Rotterdam and Düsseldorf). De Pascual and Garrido, who already swept the tie-breaks against Yanguas and Coki, have nothing to lose and plenty to gain: they are the ones who can break the script completely.

## In the women's draw, the stone in the No. 1 pair's shoe

The most awaited quarter is **Triay-Brea v Jensen-Icardo**. The world No. 1 pair defend a 2,780-point lead over Ari Sánchez and Ustero in the race for the year-end top spot, but in front of them are the only pair who have beaten them in a final this season on tour: at the Bordeaux P2, **Icardo and Jensen** took the title 3-6, 6-1, 6-1 after knocking out Josemaría and Bea in the semi-finals (6-4, 6-3). Two titles together and the pair that has read the top teams best when the match gets heavy.

The rest of the women's draw: **Ari Sánchez and Ustero v Velasco and Eugenio**, **Josemaría and Bea v Guinart and Ale Alonso** — Guinart and Alonso had just beaten Cánovas and Lobo — and **Claudia Fernández and Martina Calvo v Rufo and Iglesias**.
