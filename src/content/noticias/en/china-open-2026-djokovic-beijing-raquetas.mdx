The **China Open in Beijing** (an ATP 500 running from September 30 to October 6 at the National Tennis Center, with 500 points and $764,750 for the champion) is underway, and it has one story above all others: **Novak Djokovic is back in his fortress** eleven years after his last visit, carrying a record of 30 wins and 0 losses in the Chinese capital. And he marked the return in style, winning his first official match since Wimbledon.

## Djokovic, back in an unbeaten fortress

Six appearances in Beijing, six titles and a **30-0 record** that remains intact after his opener: on Wednesday he beat Portugal's **Nuno Borges 6-3, 7-6(2)** in one hour and 50 minutes, in his first official match since Wimbledon after more than two months off. Beijing had been waiting for his return since 2015, and the Serb is answering exactly as he always has in the city where his great Asian streak began.

His next opponent will be home wildcard **Bu Yunchaokete** (world No. 104), who knocked out **Juan Manuel Cerúndolo 6-2, 5-7, 6-4** and has the home crowd behind him. The road to the final is packed with big names, but day one already delivered a warning: **second seed Félix Auger-Aliassime fell in his debut**, 6-3, 6-3 against Karen Khachanov.

## Day one claimed an illustrious victim

Wednesday's shock came from **Karen Khachanov**, who knocked out **Auger-Aliassime (2) 6-3, 6-3**: the Canadian leaves Beijing without a win, a huge loss in a draw that also misses 2025 champion Jannik Sinner, absent with a knee injury. The rest of the winners' list was completed by Struff (6-1, 6-4 vs Tirante) and Bu Yunchaokete, Cerúndolo's conqueror (6-2, 5-7, 6-4) and Djokovic's next opponent.
