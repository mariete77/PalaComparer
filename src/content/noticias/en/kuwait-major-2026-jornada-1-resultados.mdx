The Rafa Nadal Academy Kuwait opened the main draw of the **Kuwait Major** — the first Major in Asia's history — on Sunday with a men's first round of modest label but long reach: the sixteen seeds are exempt until the round of 32, so the day was settled between qualifiers, lucky losers and the three local wild cards. The outcome: match by match, no upsets at the top, but comebacks that make a season.

## The day of the comebacks

The most painful one belonged to **Jensen-Gala**: they took the first set 6-1 against Argentines **Rubini-Chozas** and had the ticket in hand until the second set slipped away in the tiebreak. The Argentines closed it out **1-6, 7-6(5) and 6-4** in 2h20 to reach the round of 32, where Jofre-Esbrí (13) await. Down the same road went the qualifier duo **Ortega-Axelsson**: they came back on Gutiérrez-Sánchez Blasco with a **4-6, 7-6(7) and 6-3** to sneak into the round of 32. And in the late session, **Belluati-Abud** left Martínez-Hernández Quesada stranded by **3-6, 6-2 and 7-5**.

## The upset: Ruiz-López take down Aguirre-Lijó

If one result from day 1 deserves a frame, it's **Valentín Ruiz and Facundo López** beating **Leonel Aguirre and Pablo Lijó** **7-5, 0-6 and 6-2**. A statement from an unseeded pair now sitting among the last 32 of a Major. More serene passages came from **González San Martín-Cabeza** over Geens-Guichard (6-2, 6-3) and from **Abbate-Dal Bianco**, who gave Valenzuela-Gil no chance (6-3, 6-1).

## Kuwait's wild cards, on the canvas

Local padel is still learning: the **Nawaf** brothers lasted 48 minutes against Capra-Sánchez (**2-6 and 0-6**), the invitees **Redha-Miñano** fell to Goenaga-Collado (2-6, 3-6) and **Castillo-Hossam** did likewise against Perino-Domínguez (3-6, 3-6). In the women's draw, wild cards Al Qenaei-Guimet and Hasan-Atencia debut this Monday.

## The women's qualifying, with casualties

The final qualifying round took out three seeds: **Carrascosa-Fernández García** eliminated third favourites Arellano-Cortiles (5-7, 6-3, 6-4), **Bahurel-Val López** swept the fourth seeds Rose-López (6-3, 6-2), and **Dubins-Luttikhuis** needed **3 hours and 27 minutes** to knock out the seventh seeds Pérez Parra-Santos: 7-5, 5-7 and 7-6(6).

## This Monday: the favourites debut

Monday brings the first heavyweight clashes: **Stupaczuk-Sanz (4)** open the centre court against Fernández Lancha-Diestro, **Guerrero-Leal (7)** start against Mouriño-Quílez, **Di Nenno-Navarro (5)** measure Montiel-Santigosa and **González-Campagnolo (8)** face Sintes-Zamora. The women's draw begins its first round with Triay-Brea, Josemaría-González, Ari Sánchez-Ustero and Fernández-Calvo debuting on Tuesday. [**Coello and Tapia, who can virtually seal the year-end No. 1 in Kuwait**](/en/noticias/kuwait-major-2026-palas-favoritos), start directly in the round of 32.
