Professional padel's debut in the United Kingdom could not have had a better ending. The **London P1**, the first Premier Padel tournament held in the British capital, crowned two history-making pairs this Sunday at the iconic **Olympia London** in Kensington, with **20,000 fans** packing the venue throughout the week.

## Calvo and Fernández: youth takes charge

The women's pair of **Martina Calvo and Claudia Fernández** made history by becoming the youngest duo ever to win a Premier Padel tournament. Calvo, at just **18 years, one month and four days old**, entered the record books outright as the youngest player to lift a title on the circuit.

In the final, the Spaniards defeated world No. 1s **Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea** in straight sets, **7-5 and 6-3**, in one hour and 45 minutes. It was the perfect revenge after losing to the same opponents just a week earlier in the Pretoria P1 final.

<Callout title="Historic record">
Martina Calvo becomes the youngest champion in Premier Padel history, breaking every precocity record on the circuit. For Claudia Fernández, it is career title number 13 and her first of 2026.
</Callout>

The road to the final was not easy. In the semifinals, they got past world No. 2s **Bea González and Paula Josemaría** in straight sets (6-2, 6-4), repeating their Pretoria feat. They have only played two tournaments together and already have two finals under their belt: two out of two.

## Coello and Tapia: eighth title of the season

In the men's draw, **Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia** returned to winning ways after their streak was broken in Pretoria, where they fell in the semifinals. The world No. 1s defeated **Alejandro Galán and Federico Chingotto** by **6-3, 5-7 and 7-5** in one hour and 51 minutes of epic padel.

It was the thirty-ninth meeting between the two best pairs in the world, with **26 wins for Coello-Tapia**, who have also now won five consecutive clashes against Chingalán. The deciding set was resolved by a single break in the twelfth game, when a Chingotto lob sailed long and the No. 1s sealed the title.

<Callout title="A record-breaking season">
With this London title, Coello and Tapia notch up their **eighth championship of 2026** after wins in Riyadh, Cancún, Rome, Valencia, Valladolid, Bordeaux and Málaga. A crushing dominance that reinforces their hegemony in the world rankings.
</Callout>

## Padel conquers the United Kingdom

The tournament confirmed padel's international appeal. With **20,000 spectators** filling the Olympia London stands over the course of the week, the first British edition made it clear that the racket sport has taken root in a market that until now belonged to tennis and squash.

British No. 1 **Medina Murphy** was the big local attraction and shone in the main draw in what the circuit described as a historic debut. The event became an unprecedented sporting and social occasion for padel in the country.

## Prize money

The London P1, as a P1 category tournament, distributed a total prize money pool of **€479,068**. The men's champions (Coello and Tapia) earned €25,500 each, while the women's champions (Calvo and Fernández) received €17,000 each.
