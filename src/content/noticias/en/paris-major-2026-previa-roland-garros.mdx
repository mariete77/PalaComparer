The calendar gives no respite: while the **Movistar Arena** decides this Sunday the champions of the **Comunidad de Madrid P1** (Galán-Chingotto against Nieto-Yanguas in the men's draw; Triay-Brea against Josemaría-González in the women's), the **Alpine Paris Major**, one of the biggest tournaments of the season, has already started its qualifying rounds in **Paris**. From **September 6 to 13**, the Stade Roland-Garros becomes the world capital of padel for a few days: **€1,044,849** in prize money — more than double the €479,068 of the Madrid P1 — and **2,000 FIP points** for the champions. And it arrives at the worst possible moment for the No.1s, knocked out this very week in Madrid.

## Roland-Garros: tennis' temple surrenders to padel

It is the first time a Premier Padel Major is held at the French Tennis Federation's grounds, and the tournament is organized by the FFT itself. Padel takes over **six outdoor competition courts** — with MejorSet official courts, Mondo turf and Wilson Premier Padel balls — in the Paris complex.

The week's schedule:

- **Qualifying**: Sunday 6 (from 11:00) and Monday 7. 16 men's and 16 women's pairs fight for four spots in each draw.
- **Main draw**: starts Monday 7 with the men's first round; the women's side gets going on Tuesday 8.
- The 16 seeds make their debut on **Wednesday 9** in the round of 32; the round of 16 is on **Thursday 10**, the quarter-finals on **Friday 11**, the semifinals on **Saturday 12** and the **final on Sunday 13 at 2:00 pm**.

## The Race is wide open: farewell to Coello-Tapia's invincibility

The story of the week belongs to **Coki Nieto and Mike Yanguas**: in the Madrid P1 quarter-finals they knocked out **Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia** **7-5, 7-6(5)**. It's the first time since May 2025 that the world No.1s miss out on a semifinal — and it cost them dearly: the P1's 1,000 points slipped away right before the tournament that hands out the most points of the season's second half.

**Alejandro Galán and Federico Chingotto** also went to the wire in Madrid: they edged out **Martín Di Nenno and Paquito Navarro** — the reigning Madrid champions — **6-4, 3-6, 7-6(5)** and today play the Madrid title against the upset-makers who beat the No.1s.

The **Race** is therefore blown wide open: Coello-Tapia's lead stood at **1,290 points** on August 24 (10,440 vs 9,150), and now **2,000** are on offer in Paris. What's more, the No.1s defend at Roland-Garros the title they won in the 2025 edition: a chance for revenge twice over.

## The draw: favourites and unseeded threats

The top eight men's seeds are **Coello-Tapia, Galán-Chingotto, Lebrón-Augsburger, Stupa-Sanz, Nieto-Yanguas, Navarro-Di Nenno, Guerrero-Leal and González-Campagnolo**. The third seeds, **Juan Lebrón and Leo Augsburger**, look for redemption in Paris after their early Madrid loss to **Gonza Alfonso and Tino Libaak**, who are unseeded yet already showed in the Movistar Arena they can beat anyone: they made the semifinals. The draw is completed by three wild cards for French padel (including **Yoan Boronad** and **Jeremy Robert**) and four pairs out of qualifying.

## The catalog rackets being played in Paris

- **Arturo Coello** — **Head Coello Pro 2026**: the first model of his signature collection with Head, with the **Coello Motion 2026** as the more manoeuvrable alternative.
- **Agustín Tapia** — **NOX AT10 Genius 18K**: 18K carbon and that blend of control and ball exit with which he has dominated the Race.
- **Alejandro Galán** — **Adidas Metalbone 2026**: high-balance diamond built for attack, with the **Metalbone HRD+** as the more demanding version.
- **Fede Chingotto** — **Bullpadel Neuron 02**: hybrid with a 3K carbon face at around 359 grams, precise on the backhand.
- **Paquito Navarro** — **Bullpadel Hack 04 2026**, the "Dale Candela": high-balance diamond and 18K carbon.
- **Tino Libaak** — **Siux Diablo Pro 2026**: teardrop in 18K TeXtreme carbon with 3D texture, the model designed for him.
- Women's side: **Delfi Brea** plays with the **Bullpadel Vertex 05 Woman 2026**, the Vertex family's diamond mold lightened (350-360 g) for more manoeuvrability.

## Women's draw: Triay-Brea defend their crown

On the women's side, **Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea** defend in Paris the title they won in the 2025 edition and arrive after reaching today's Madrid final (6-2, 6-4 against Sánchez-Ustero in the semifinals). Their biggest rivals, **Paula Josemaría and Bea González**, got there the hard way in the Movistar Arena: they came from behind against **Claudia Fernández and Martina Calvo** (4-6, 6-2, 7-5), the London champions who had won their first two tournaments together. The youngest pair ever to win a Premier Padel title wants revenge at Roland-Garros.
