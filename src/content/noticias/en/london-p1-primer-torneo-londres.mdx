The professional padel circuit lands in **London** this week: the **London P1** is the first Premier Padel tournament ever staged in the British capital, taking place at the iconic **Olympia London** in Kensington.

## A historic week for padel in the UK

The main draw starts this week with the world's top-ranked players. The event follows the circuit's debut in South Africa, where **Triay-Brea** and **Galán-Chingotto** took the titles in Pretoria after two finals decided in third-set tie-breaks.

The event is a clear bet on padel's growth in the UK, one of the sport's emerging markets, with a fanbase that keeps growing tournament after tournament.

## Upcoming tour stops

The tour doesn't stop: after London comes **Madrid P1** in late August, the **Paris Major** in September and the season finale with the **Premier Padel Finals** in December, where Fanatics will launch in-venue retail.
