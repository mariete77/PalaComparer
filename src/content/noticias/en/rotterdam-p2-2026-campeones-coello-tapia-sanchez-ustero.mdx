The **CUPRA Rotterdam Premier Padel P2** ended on Sunday at the Rotterdam Ahoy with the same script as the Paris Major: **the two pairs crowned at Roland Garros repeated in the Netherlands**. **Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero** took the women's title with a **6-4 6-4** win over Paula Josemaría and Bea González (1h41), while **Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia** defended their men's crown by beating Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz **6-3 6-2** (1h09). Each pair earned **600 points** for the FIP Ranking and the Race to the Finals in Barcelona.

## Ari Sánchez turns her 100th final into the sweetest title

At 29, the Catalan is **the youngest player in padel history to reach 100 professional finals** — the day before she had beaten the No. 1 seeds, **Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea, 6-2 6-3** in the semi-finals — and on Sunday she rounded off the milestone with victory: a second consecutive title over the No. 2 seeds after the Paris Major, and the **pair's fourth trophy of the season**.

> "Winning two finals in a row was a goal: this title means so much. I had some difficult moments during the match, but Andrea was extraordinary: she is getting stronger and stronger," said Ari. Her partner, Andrea Ustero, added: "It was not easy to beat the top two seeds at the same tournament, but this should give us even more motivation to keep working."

Josemaría and González, chasing a seventh title of the year (against Triay-Brea's six), recovered both breaks immediately but never managed to get ahead. They remain level at the top of the women's Race with the No. 1s, barely 200 points ahead of Sánchez-Ustero.

## Tapia-Coello: a tenth title of the year and a perfect record at the Ahoy

The world No. 1s **defend their 2025 Rotterdam title** and add their **tenth trophy of the 2026 season**, stretching their lead over Galán-Chingotto in the FIP Race ahead of three consecutive European tournaments. Stupa-Sanz's fast start hinted at a closer final, but the Golden Boys pulled away with fewer unforced errors (22 to 20) and many more winners (**41 against 27**).

## Stupa-Sanz: a dream run, one step short

Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz, a pair for only a few weeks, completed a remarkable month: gold at the FIP Gold in Belgrade in their first week together, a final at the FIP Platinum in Lyon and now **their first Premier Padel final as a pair**, beating Galán-Chingotto in the quarters and their former partners Yanguas-Nieto (6-0 6-4) in the semis. The defeat didn't spoil the good news: they secured **No. 4 pair status on the Tour** for the two remaining events of the European swing.
