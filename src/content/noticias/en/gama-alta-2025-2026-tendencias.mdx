This analysis comes from the data we hold in the catalog: 48 models across
padel and tennis, from 2022 to 2026. It's not a market survey — it's what shows
up when you sort our own spec sheets.

## Padel: 18K is no longer exclusive

Three years ago carbon 18K was reserved for two or three top-tier models. Today
it shows up across the whole €250-and-up band, and brands lead with it as a
selling point ahead of the core.

That's a problem for the buyer: the face weave matters less than the rubber for
final feel, but it's what goes big on the box. We dig into that in the
[carbon guide](/noticias/carbono-3k-12k-18k-diferencias).

<ProductRef id="nox-at10-genius-attack-18k-2026" /> and
<ProductRef id="babolat-technical-viper-2025" /> are the exponents of that
drift: 18K or equivalent, diamond and hard rubber, all three at once.

## Diamond dominates signature paddles

Among models with a player attached in the catalog, most are diamond or hybrid
leaning diamond. It makes commercial sense — those are the smashers' paddles on
tour — but it explains why so many people buy a paddle that doesn't match their
game.

<Callout title="Practical read">
A paddle carrying a pro's name says nothing about whether it suits you. It says
what kind of game that person plays. Look at the shape and balance, not the
signature.
</Callout>

## The "comfort" tier appears

The most interesting thing in recent years isn't 18K: it's the softened
versions of aggressive models.
<ProductRef id="bullpadel-vertex-04-comfort-2024" /> keeps the original's shape
but drops the hardness, and the "Light" lines brands have been adding to their
top ranges go the same way.

It's the brands' answer to elbow injuries, and probably the most useful trend of
the last two years for the average player.

## Tennis: everything converges on 100 inches

In the tennis catalog, the 100 in² head size has eaten the entire mid-range.
The 95-98 in² survives only in the pure-control tier
(<ProductRef id="wilson-pro-staff-97-v14-2023" />), and oversize heads are
relegated to beginners.

<ProductRef id="head-speed-mp-2024" /> is the archetype of the current standard:
100 in², 16x19 pattern and mid-zone swingweight.
