The **Davis Cup Final 8 field is now complete**. Between September 18 and 20, seven ties handed out the tickets to **Bologna (November 24-29)**, where defending champions Italy wait as hosts. Joining the Italians will be **Spain, Germany, Great Britain, Austria, Canada, Czechia and South Korea** — and the weekend produced two headline stories: **Czechia knocked the United States out in Prague** and **Spain won in Chile without dropping a single rubber**, and without Alcaraz. (Sources: daviscup.com, AP, worldtennismagazine.com, Tennis Canada, tennistemple, RFI, T13.)

## Prague: Czechia come from behind to eliminate the US Open runner-up

The most anticipated tie delivered the drama. The United States took a **2-1** lead: after Friday's 1-1 (**Tien d. Mensik 6-2 6-4** and **Lehecka d. Shelton 6-4 6-4**), the doubles pairing of **Harrison-Krajicek** put the Americans ahead while **saving four match points**. But Czechia swept Saturday's reverse singles: **Lehecka beat Tien 6-3 7-5** and **Mensik came from behind to beat Shelton 5-7 6-4 6-3**, sealing the **3-2** at Prague's O2 Arena.

For **Ben Shelton**, it has been a week to remember for the wrong reasons: US Open runner-up on Sunday the 13th, then two losses in his two Davis Cup rubbers, the second with his **Yonex EZONE 98** — <ProductRef id="yonex-ezone-98-2025" /> —, the frame behind his best Grand Slam run. The United States, the nation with the most Salad Bowls (32), now **extends its title drought to 19 years** and misses the Final 8 via the qualifying path. On the Czech side, **Jakub Mensik** sticks with the **Wilson Blade 98 v10** — <ProductRef id="wilson-blade-98-v10-2026" /> — while Lehecka stays loyal to his **Six.One 95**.

## Quebec City: Auger-Aliassime, hero of the city's first Davis Cup since 1975

Canada delivered in front of history: **3-1 over France** at the Centre Videotron. **Liam Draxl** opened with the biggest win of his career over **Arthur Rinderknech (6-2 7-5)**, and **Felix Auger-Aliassime** edged **Quentin Halys** in the night session (**7-6(5) 4-6 6-3**). France clung on through the doubles — **Bonzi-Herbert d. Chan-FAA 7-6(4) 6-3** — but on Saturday the hometown boy turned hero: **FAA came back to beat Rinderknech 4-6 7-6(4) 6-4** and sealed the ticket in front of his own crowd with the **2026 Babolat Pure Aero 98** — <ProductRef id="babolat-pure-aero-98-2026" /> —, the same frame family as Alcaraz and Rune. His [player page](/jugadores/felix-auger-aliassime) is already in the catalog.

## Halle: Zverev delivers the final blow to Croatia, a week after the US Open

Germany needed Sunday to see off Croatia (**3-2** at the heristo-arena). The doubles was the key: **Krawietz-Puetz beat Pavic-Mektic — the Olympic gold-medal pair from Tokyo — 7-5 6-2**. And the finishing touch came from **Alexander Zverev**, who **seven days after lifting the US Open** beat **Dino Prizmic 6-2 6-4** to confirm Germany's spot. The world No. 2 heads to Bologna with the **Head Gravity Tour Zverev 2026** — <ProductRef id="head-gravity-tour-zverev-2026" /> —, the limited edition he won Roland Garros and New York with (with a [player page](/jugadores/alexander-zverev) and catalog entry of its own).

## Santiago: Spain, without Alcaraz and without dropping a rubber

**David Ferrer's** team completed a clean sweep: **4-0 over Chile** at the Estadio Nacional, **without losing a single rubber**, and a third straight Final 8. Rafael Jodar enjoyed the dream debut — **6-0 6-2 over Cristian Garin** in barely over an hour, part of a 20-wins-in-24-matches clay record in 2026 — and **Daniel Merida** came from behind to beat **Alejandro Tabilo (5-7 6-3 6-3)** to make it 2-0. The doubles **Pedro Martinez-Jaume Munar** clinched the tie **6-0 6-3**, and the 4-0 was the final flourish. Jodar, the great sensation of Spanish tennis, plays with the **HEAD Speed MP 2026** — <ProductRef id="head-speed-mp-2026" /> —, already in the catalog with his [player page](/jugadores/rafael-jodar). The only question for November is the familiar one: **whether Carlos Alcaraz joins the team** in Bologna.

## Express tickets: London, Seoul and Vienna

The other three places came with less suspense. **Great Britain thrashed Ecuador 4-0** at the Copper Box — their first Final 8 since 2023 — with a winning debut for **Toby Samuel (4-6 6-4 6-1 over Andrade)**, **Arthur Fery's 7-5 6-0** and the **Patten-Skupski** doubles sealing the series. In Seoul, **South Korea beat India 3-1** with **Kwon Soon-woo** clinching the place against Sumit Nagal (**6-1 6-2**); and in Vienna, **Austria beat Belgium 3-1** with **Jurij Rodionov** as Zizou Bergs' executioner (**7-6(6) 6-3**). Italy, meanwhile, prepares its defence at home: **four straight titles** would be their record in the team competition.

Outside the Qualifiers, a gear note from the World Group I: **Holger Rune** returned to Davis Cup after eleven months and carried Denmark past Bulgaria (**3-2**), winning the decider against **Grigor Dimitrov (3-6 6-3 7-6(4))** with his **Pure Aero 98** — the same frame as the [catalog entry](/producto/babolat-pure-aero-98-2026).
