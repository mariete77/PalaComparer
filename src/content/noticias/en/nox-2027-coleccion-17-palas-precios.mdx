**NOX** has officially unveiled its 2027 collection, a **17-racket** range organised in four families —**Genius, Ventus, Pro Cup and Equation Advanced**— and presented under the **"100% Pure Padel"** campaign, with **Agustín Tapia** and **Miguel Lamperti** as its faces since 23 September. The full catalogue is already live on the official website, with prices running from the **€124.99** Equation Advanced to the **€389.99** top of the range, and most models open for pre-order.

The structure is straightforward: **five Genius** rackets (all Tapia's), **five Ventus** signed by Edu Alonso, Aimar Goñi, Lamperti and Aranzazu Osoro, **two more Pro Cups** from Tapia plus **three ML10 Pro Cups** from Lamperti, and **two Equation Advanced** models closing the range below €130.

## Genius: five rackets for the world No. 1

**Agustín Tapia's** range keeps the architecture of recent years but sharpens it. The **Genius 18K Alum** (€389.99) is the all-round option: teardrop shape, **18K Alum** carbon faces and an **MLD Black EVA** core, at 360-375 g. Its aggressive sibling, the **Genius Attack 18K Alum**, keeps the materials but moves to a **diamond shape** and high balance for those who live at the net. Both are repeated in **12K Alum XTREM** versions at the same €389.99 price, with a weave NOX describes as more consistent through temperature changes. The fifth model is the **Genius 12K Alum XTREM Lite** (€349.99), which drops to 350-360 g and switches to a softer **HR3 White EVA** core for players who want the premium range in a lighter format.

> That gives Tapia **seven rackets** bearing his signature in 2027: five Genius models plus the Pro Cup HARD and Pro Cup SOFT.

## Ventus: the family that keeps growing

NOX's big bet for 2027 sits with the **Ventus** line, now five rackets strong. **Edu Alonso's Ventus Hybrid 12K XTREM** (€349.99) is the versatile teardrop option, while **Aimar Goñi's Ventus Attack 12K XTREM** — one of the breakout signings of the season — matches the price with a diamond shape and an **Oversize Grip extended by 30 mm**, designed for two-handed power shots. **Lamperti's Ventus Control 3K** (€349.99) targets control with **3K carbon** faces, and **Aranzazu Osoro's Ventus Control 12K** (€299.99) is the most accessible round-shape option of the series. The **Ventus Hybrid 12K Lite** (€299.99, 350-360 g) completes the family for those who prefer low weight without stepping down a tier.

## Pro Cup and Equation: the entry point

At **€199.99** sit **Tapia's Pro Cup HARD and SOFT**: teardrop shape, aluminised fibreglass and two HR3 hardness options to choose your impact feel. **Lamperti's three ML10 Pro Cups** (€149.99) — COORP, Rough and Silver — keep the classic round shape, aluminised fibreglass and a replaceable Smart Strap. And the **Equation HARD and SOFT Advanced** (€124.99) are the gateway: round moulds, 3K fibreglass, 3D texture and 355-365 g.

## The technologies behind the collection

The 2027 range strengthens several fronts. The **VCS** vibration-absorption system arrives improved — NOX claims it filters up to **60% more** vibration than the previous version, though the figure comes from the manufacturer. **Weight Balance** lets you play with **interchangeable 2 g and 4 g counterweights** (up to 8 g in total) to tune mass and balance to taste. The **EOS Tunnel** modifies the mould openings to influence airflow and handling, and **Dual Spin** combines 3D relief with a sandblasted finish for extra spin.

## Which one should you choose?

If you are after **control and progression**, the round Equations and ML10 Pro Cups are the natural starting point, stepping up to the Ventus Control 12K if you want carbon faces. For **all-round play**, the teardrop Genius models (18K Alum or 12K Alum XTREM) and the Ventus Hybrid are the standard. And if your home is the net, the diamond **Attack** versions — Genius or Ventus — are the most demanding but also the most devastating.

Before deciding, remember that declared weight is not everything: balance and how the racket distributes its mass shape the feel in your hand far more.
