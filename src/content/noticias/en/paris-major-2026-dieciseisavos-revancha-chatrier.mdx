Two days of round-of-64 matches were enough for the **Alpine Paris Major** to start showing what it is: a 2,000-FIP-point tournament with traps in every corner of the draw. Qualifying squeezed four pairs into each main draw, the French wild cards said goodbye at home —Boronad-Robert fell 7-5, 6-4 to Rubini-Piotto— and the draw has prepared a venomous debut for the No. 1s: **Agustín Tapia and Arturo Coello open today on Philippe Chatrier court (12:00, Spanish time) against Tino Libaak and Gonza Alfonso**, the unseeded pair that knocked Lebrón-Augsburger out of the Madrid semifinal race at the Movistar Arena and reached the last four, and that in Paris got past Lijó-Gil (6-4, 3-6, 6-1) to earn the date.

## How we got here: two days of upsets

**Monday 7.** The first men's session had a bit of everything. **Tolito Aguirre and Pablo García** cruised past Miguel and Nuno Deus (6-2, 6-2), **Fede Mouriño and Marc Quílez** beat Santigosa-Pereyra (6-1, 7-5), and the two tightest matches went the distance: **Patiniotis-Diego García** survived a tie-break marathon (7-6(4), 6(6)-7, 7-6(5)) and **Vilariño-Zamora** got past Perino-Fernández Lancha (6-3, 3-6, 6-3).

**Tuesday 8.** The day of local farewells. **Jesús Moya and Thomas Leygue**, the home hope, saved an agonising first-set tie-break against Gala-Jensen (7-6(8), 6-4); fellow Frenchmen **Clément Geens and Dylan Guichard** hammered Pineda-Ruiz (6-0, 6-2); and the upset came from **Arnau Ayats and Emilio Sánchez Chamero**, who eliminated **Maxi Sánchez and Lucho Capra** in straight sets (7-6(3), 6-2). In the women's draw, **Alix Collombon**, the great figure of French padel, said goodbye alongside Ksenia Sharifova against Arellano-Lobo (3-6, 6(4)-7), and the first round spared no local dreams: Godallier-Valenzuela, Riera-Bidahorria and Clascà-Touly also went out.

## Today on Chatrier: the giants debut (with history attached)

Wednesday's order of play spreads the seeds' debut across four courts, and the show court keeps the best for itself:

- **12:00 — Tapia-Coello vs Libaak-Alfonso.** The match of the day. The No. 1s defend in Paris the title they won in 2025 —their third in a row at the Major— and arrive after their first big stumble of the season in Madrid; on the other side, the unseeded pair that already took down Lebrón-Augsburger at the Movistar Arena. Libaak and his **Siux Diablo Pro** against the Coello-Tapia wall: few round-of-32 debuts have looked this good on paper.
- **Not before 14:30 — Geens-Guichard vs Lebrón-Augsburger.** The third seeds want quick redemption after their early Madrid exit: the Frenchmen come from a 6-0, 6-2 demolition and will be no walk in the park.
- **Not before 20:00 — Ayats-Sánchez Chamero vs Galán-Chingotto.** The Madrid champions debut at night against the slayers of Maxi Sánchez-Lucho Capra. **Stupaczuk-Sanz** (vs Facu López-Dal Bianco, court 7, not before 16:00) and **Navarro-Di Nenno** (vs Collado-Hernández, not before 17:00) round out the Spanish favourites' afternoon.

## Women's draw: the favourites start on Thursday in the round of 16

While the men play their round of 32, the top women's favourites rest: **Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea**, Madrid champions and defending Paris titlists, start this **Thursday 10** directly in the round of 16 waiting for an opponent. **Paula Josemaría and Bea González** also arrive with a bitter taste after the final lost at the Movistar Arena. On the outside courts, however, this Wednesday does guarantee Spanish duels: **Lucía Sainz and Sofía Saiz** against Aida Martínez-Natividad López (court 5, to follow) and **Marta Ortega and Sofia Araújo** against Ari Cañellas-Patricia Martínez (court 7, 11:00).
