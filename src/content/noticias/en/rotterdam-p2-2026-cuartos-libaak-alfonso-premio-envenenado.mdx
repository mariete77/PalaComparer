Today, **Friday October 2**, the **CUPRA Rotterdam Premier Padel P2** (Rotterdam Ahoy, September 28 – October 4, €264,534 in prize money and 500 FIP points for the champions) plays its quarterfinals: eight matches —four men's and four women's— from **10:30 Spanish time** (the same in the Netherlands; 05:30 in Argentina), with Red Bull TV starting its broadcast of the tournament precisely in this round. Thursday's round of 16 served one big upset — the rest of the favourites delivered. This is the last-eight picture in the Ahoy.

## The round of 16 left a single outsider: Libaak-Alfonso

The story of Thursday has two names: **Tino Libaak and Gonza Alfonso** knocked out the seventh seeds, **Fran Guerrero and Javi Leal, 7-5, 3-6, 6-4**, after more than two hours of play. The Argentine pair, who had already won their main-draw debut on Tuesday against Rodríguez Martínez-Abbate, are now the **only pair outside the top eight seeds left in the quarterfinals** —and their reward is a frightening one: the world No. 1s and defending champions, **Tapia-Coello**, who began their title defence by beating Montiel-Santigosa 6-3, 6-2. The stakes don't end there: for Libaak and Alfonso, every win also strengthens their case for Argentina's squad at the World Cup in Doha (November 2-7).

The remaining favourites delivered with authority in their debuts (all of them arrived with a first-round bye):

- **Di Nenno-Paquito Navarro (4)** beat Zapata-Naranjo **6-0, 6-3** in under an hour: the new fourth-seeded pair gave nothing away.
- **Galán-Chingotto (2)** wasted no time either against Jensen-Gala: **6-0, 6-2**.
- **Augsburger-Lebrón (3)** edged Arce-Tello **6-1, 7-6(7)**.
- **Stupaczuk-Sanz (5)** sent Hernández-Castaño packing with a **double 6-2** — their second in a row after their main-draw debut.
- **Yanguas-Nieto (6)** beat Lijó-Gil **7-6(3), 6-4**.
- **Momo-Campagnolo (8)** defeated Diestro-Sintes **6-4, 7-5**.

## Women's draw: withdrawals and one surprise

In the women's draw the seeds came through comfortably, but the day was not without drama:

- **Triay-Brea (1)**, the defending champions, opened their tournament by beating Talaván-Castelló **6-1, 6-3**.
- **Josemaría-González (2)** defeated Collombon-Sharifova **6-4, 6-2**.
- **Fernández-Calvo (4)** needed just three games: Goenaga retired with hip trouble at 0-3.
- **Sánchez-Ustero (3)** advanced without playing: Sainz-Saiz withdrew at the last minute due to Lucía Sainz's physical issues.
- **Salazar-Osoro** needed three sets to get past Barrera-Montes (**7-5, 5-7, 6-1**).
- **Ortega-Araújo** ousted Godallier-Valenzuela **6-3, 7-5**.
- The surprise of the day came from **Riera-Bidahorria**: they eliminated the sixth seeds, Guinart-Alonso, **6-3, 6(3)-7, 6-3**, extending the run they arrived in the Netherlands with, after two consecutive CUPRA FIP Tour titles.
- **Jensen-Escacena** completed the list by beating Rufo-Iglesias (**6-4, 7-5**), handing Claudia Escacena her first Premier Padel quarterfinal.

## This afternoon's matches

- **Center Court**: Fernández-Calvo vs Jensen-Escacena (10:30); then Ortega-Araújo vs Josemaría-González and Di Nenno-Navarro vs Momo-Campagnolo; **not before 14:30, Tapia-Coello vs Libaak-Alfonso**; not before 18:30, Triay-Brea vs Salazar-Osoro; then Galán-Chingotto vs Stupaczuk-Sanz and Augsburger-Lebrón vs Yanguas-Nieto.
- **Court 2**: Riera-Bidahorria vs Sánchez-Ustero from 15:00.
