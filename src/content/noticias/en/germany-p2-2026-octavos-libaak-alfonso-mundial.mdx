Düsseldorf is the centre of the padel world once again. The **CUPRA Germany Premier Padel P2** — the second edition at the **CASTELLO Düsseldorf**, with the main draw running from 6 to 11 October and €264,534 in prize money — stages its big day today: the **round of 16**, 16 matches (eight men's and eight women's) from **09:00** (04:00 in Argentina) and the debuts of the big favourites. In the men's draw, the top four seeds finally enter the fray: Rotterdam's reigning champions **Tapia-Coello**; the defending champions in Germany, **Galán-Chingotto**; **Lebrón-Augsburger** and **Stupa-Sanz**. And in the women's draw, the top eight seeds make their bow, led by the 2025 champions **Triay-Brea**, No. 2 seeds **Josemaría-González** and Paris and Rotterdam winners **Sánchez-Ustero** and **Fernández-Calvo**.

The first round, spread across Tuesday and Wednesday, was anything but quiet: upsets, marathons and a story to tell. And above all, a perfect day for two Argentinians.

## Libaak-Alfonso's perfect day

**Tino Libaak and Gonza Alfonso** lived one of those days you keep forever on Wednesday. Just as they walked onto the Castello's centre court, the Argentine Padel Association released its eight-man squad for the **Doha World Cup** (2-7 November): both were in. It will be the first time Alfonso represents Argentina at a World Cup and he could not hide his emotion: "Today I received one of the greatest honours anyone can receive in their career: representing my country. My life is going to change from this World Cup onwards," he said in a video. Libaak, the hero of the 2024 final alongside Augsburger, is back in the squad.

And they celebrated it on court with the comeback of the day. The Argentinians lost the first set **3-6** to the seventh seeds, **Fran Guerrero and Javi Leal** — the same pair they had knocked out a week earlier in Rotterdam — turned the second around in the tie-break (**7-6(2)**) and found the decisive break in the 11th game of the third to close it out **7-5** after **2h23**. Today they chase the quarterfinals against **Solano-Pereyra**: it would be their second consecutive P2 quarterfinal, after Rotterdam, where they even took a set off Tapia-Coello.

## From elimination to heaven in a few hours

The other big story of the first round belonged to the kings of second chances: **Álvaro Montiel and Dani Santigosa**. The Italian-Spanish pair lost Tuesday morning's final qualifying round to Geens-Moragues in a battle lasting over two hours, and in the afternoon they were handed a main-draw spot as *lucky losers* after Coki Nieto's withdrawal — an injury that also ruled Mike Yanguas out of the tournament. That night, with tired legs, they walked back on court and **knocked out the tenth seeds, Bergamini-Bautista, who were debuting as a pair on Tour** (7-5, 7-6), in the last slot on Court 3. It is the second P2 in a row in which they reach the round of 16: today they face the veteran pair **Sanyo Gutiérrez-Maxi Sánchez**, who beat Gala-Jensen 7-6, 6-1.

The rest of the first round looked like this:

- **Momo-Campagnolo, the first big casualty**: the Rotterdam semi-finalists and eighth seeds fell in their opening match to the new Argentine pair **Arce-Tello** (7-5, 7-6). Maxi Arce, who also made the World Cup squad, keeps his bid for the Barcelona Finals alive alongside Juan Tello. Today they face Geens-Moragues.
- **Esbrí-Jofre win the arm-wrestle**: 4-6, 6-3, 7-6 against Goenaga-Collado in another two-hour-plus battle. Their reward is today's standout match: **Lebrón-Augsburger**, the third seeds.
- **Paquito-Di Nenno deliver**: 6-4, 6-4 against Goñi-Alonso; today they meet the young pair **Castaño-Pol Hernández**, who saw off Arroyo-Jiménez 6-3, 6-2.
- **Garrido-De Pascual's winning debut**: Javi Garrido and Juani De Pascual debuted as a Premier Padel pair beating Ruiz-García in straight sets. The reward is huge: **Galán-Chingotto**.
- **Three qualifying pairs remain alive** in the men's draw — Geens-Moragues, Solano-Pereyra and Martínez-Hernández — although the German wild cards (Wunner-Lindmeyer, Reca-Tur and Plümer-Hering) bowed out at home. In the women's draw, whose first round was played on Wednesday, **Eugenio-Velasco** produced the comeback of the day against Collombon-Sharifova (4-6, 7-6, 6-0 in 2h16) and **Aguilar-Escacena** confirmed their ticket from qualifying.
