Today, **Sunday, October 11**, the **CUPRA Germany Premier Padel P2** (Castello Düsseldorf, €264,534 in prize money) wraps up its week with finals day: two matches on Centre Court from **14:00 Spanish time (09:00 in Argentina)**, both on **Red Bull TV**. Both finals come with their own story: the men's is **the exact rematch of last week's Rotterdam final**, and the women's pits the circuit's most in-form pair against the pair behind the comeback of the season.

## Tapia-Coello vs Stupa-Sanz: the Rotterdam rematch

Saturday's men's semi-finals put the favourites in the final, but through very different paths. **Agustín Tapia and Arturo Coello** left no room for **Juan Lebrón and Leo Augsburger**: **6-2 6-3 in 1h05**, their third win of the week (after 6-2 6-3 over Diestro-Sintes and 6-2 7-5 over Alfonso-Libaak in the quarter-finals). The No. 1s reach their **third straight final** — after the Paris Major and the Rotterdam P2 — with a **69-7 record in the 2026 season**.

On the other side are **Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz**, who once again beat **Ale Galán and Fede Chingotto 7-6(3) 6-4** (1h56, closing the night in Düsseldorf). It is their **second straight win over the No. 2s**, after the 6-4 6-4 in the Rotterdam quarter-finals. The 2025 champions in Germany miss out on a repeat and miss a Premier Padel final for the second straight tournament.

Today's final is, literally, last week's in Rotterdam Ahoy: Tapia-Coello already beat Stupa-Sanz **6-3 6-2 in Rotterdam** — their tenth title of 2026 — and lead **1-0 in their head-to-head as a pair**. What's at stake: the Golden Boys chase an **11th title of the year** and the Paris-Rotterdam-Düsseldorf European treble; Stupa and Jon go for their **first title together** after three straight finals (they lost the FIP Platinum Lyon final and the Rotterdam one). "We like playing on Sundays! I hope this win is the start of something big," Stupa warned after the Rotterdam semi-finals.

## Sánchez-Ustero chase the treble; Calvo-Fernández pulled off the comeback of the year

In the women's draw, **Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero** once again knocked out the No. 1s: **7-5 3-6 7-5 over Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea**, for the third time this season in a semi-final (after Valladolid and Rotterdam). Paris Major and Rotterdam champions, the pair chase today their **third straight title and fifth of the season**.

Their opponents will be **Martina Calvo and Claudia Fernández**, authors of Saturday's epic: **6-4 5-7 7-6(5) in 3h07** against Paula Josemaría and Bea González, **saving two match points** in a battle decided in the third-set tie-break. The London P1 champions — the youngest pair ever to win a Premier Padel title — thus take revenge for the Rotterdam semi-finals, where Josemaría-González had beaten them 6-3 6-4.
