The round of 16 in Acapulco took more than a handful of seeds with it: **it closed the Premier Padel Finals door on four players**. **Coki Nieto, Momo González, Edu Alonso and Aimar Goñi** can no longer be at the Palau Sant Jordi on 10-13 December, and the same is true in the women's draw for **Aranza Osoro, Nuria Rodríguez and Giulia Dal Pozzo**.

What is still open is one ticket. And it belongs to two players who meet tonight: **Javi Leal and Tino Libaak**.

## The round of 16 that broke the Race

The hardest blow came from **Íñigo Jofre and Juanlu Esbrí**. **Coki Nieto and Mike Yanguas** (5) took the first set **6-3**, looked in control, and then lost their grip: two near-identical sets, **6-2 and 6-2**, in a little over two hours. Coki arrived in Acapulco still doing the maths on the Race. He leaves with nothing left to calculate.

**Momo González and Lucas Campagnolo** (8) went out cleanly against **Jairo Bautista and Lucas Bergamini** (11), **7-5 and 6-1**, and said goodbye to Barcelona. **Edu Alonso and Aimar Goñi** are out too: they lost the longest match of the day to **Leal and Guerrero**, **7-6, 6-7 and 6-2** after **two hours and forty minutes**, with a measure of revenge for the Kuwait Major thrown in.

The third heavyweight exit was **Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz** (3), beaten **7-6, 4-6 and 6-4** by **Libaak and Alfonso** in a match settled in the last few games. Sanz had already booked his Finals place on Wednesday. His partner had not.

## The ticket that is decided tonight

The numbers Premier Padel published before the tournament put the cut at **3,310 points**, Momo González's total as 16th. **Javi Leal** arrived on **3,237** (73 short) and **Tino Libaak** on **3,235** (75 short). Both won their round of 16, both are still alive, and now they play each other.

In plain terms: **whoever wins tonight is in**. Whoever loses spends the off-season looking at the calendar. And there is a detail that adds spice: **Gonza Alfonso**, Libaak's partner, already had his place; **Fran Guerrero**, Leal's partner, has his too. Both pairs are competing with one ticket inside and one outside.

Libaak and Alfonso did not get here by accident. Since teaming up in July they **have not gone out before the quarter-finals in seven straight events**, and in Acapulco they have already removed one of the three best pairs in the draw.

## Valenzuela and Gil, the story of the Major

If one pair has changed category in Acapulco, it is **Ramiro Valenzuela and Xisco Gil**. Unseeded, they knocked out **Maxi Arce and Juan Tello** (7) **7-6 and 7-5** in the round of 32, then came from a set down against **Marc Sintes and Coquito Zamora**, saving a tense tie-break (**9-7**) before running away with the third: **3-6, 7-6 and 6-2**.

It is **their first Major quarter-final**. The prize is the worst possible one: **Tapia and Coello** on centre court. The world number ones dropped a set against **Álex Ruiz and Javi García** (**6-1, 6-7 and 6-0**) and are still chasing a clean sweep of all four Majors this season, after Rome, Paris and Kuwait.

## The other half: Lebrón and Di Nenno, no alarms

**Juan Lebrón and Leo Augsburger** (2) got past **Maxi Sánchez Blasco and Sanyo Gutiérrez** **7-6 and 6-4**, and in the quarter-finals they meet the men who ended Coki's run: **Jofre and Esbrí**, a pair arriving with momentum and nothing to lose.

**Martín Di Nenno and Paquito Navarro** (4) produced the cleanest win of the night against **José Jiménez and Álex Chozas** (**6-3 and 6-4**) and face **Bautista and Bergamini**, fresh from beating Momo and Campagnolo.

## Women's draw: Salazar's farewell, in Triay and Brea's hands

In the women's draw the story is even crueller. **Bea Caldera and Carmen Goenaga** beat **Ale Salazar and Aranza Osoro** **6-3 and 6-4**, a result that puts Osoro out of the Finals. Salazar, who retires from professional padel in Barcelona, **no longer controls her own fate**: she qualifies if **Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea** beat Caldera and Goenaga tonight.

And the world number ones have their own score to settle: **if they reach the final, they seal the year-end number one for a second straight season**. In the round of 16 they suffered against **Jimena Velasco and Raquel Eugenio** (**3-6, 6-3 and 6-3**), almost two and a half hours to close a match they started by losing.

The rest of the favourites went through smoothly. **Paula Josemaría and Bea González** won **6-0 and 7-5**; **Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero**, **6-1 and 6-3**; **Martina Calvo and Claudia Fernández**, **6-3 and 6-2**. **Marina Guinart and Ale Alonso** already have their Barcelona ticket and celebrated with a **6-1 and 6-3**.
