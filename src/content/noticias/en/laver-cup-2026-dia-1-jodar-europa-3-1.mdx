The ninth Laver Cup is underway at **The O2 in London (September 25-27)** and the opening day delivered a script full of Spanish tennis: **Europe lead 3-1** over Team World after **Rafa Jódar** steamrolled Bublik on his debut and **Carlos Alcaraz and Jakub Menšík** closed Friday with a doubles win. Friday victories were worth one point each; Saturday ones will be worth **two** and Sunday ones **three**, with the trophy going to the first team to reach 13. (Sources: lavercup.com, ABC, Mundo Deportivo, AS, Infobae, Eurosport.)

## Ruud opens with a comeback: 6-7(4), 6-4, 10-8 over Cerúndolo

The Norwegian opened the tournament for a **fifth time** and got his revenge against the only player to have beaten him in Laver Cup singles: Cerúndolo, in the 2024 opener in Berlin. The Argentine controlled the first hour, but Ruud — with 33 unforced errors to just 15 winners — found a way through a 2h06 comeback decided in the **Laver Breaker** (10-8) after saving 0-40 when his rival served at 4-4 in the second set. The win came with the Norwegian's **Yonex EZONE 100** — in our catalog you'll find its 98 sq-in sister frame, the <ProductRef id="yonex-ezone-98-2025" /> played by Shelton and Pegula —, the family Ruud has trusted for years.

## Nakashima answers for the World: the VCORE 98 stops Menšík

Team World's first point came from debutant **Brandon Nakashima**, who joined the team after Ben Shelton's late withdrawal. The Californian came from behind to beat Czech **Jakub Menšík** 1-6, 6-3, 10-7 in the Laver Breaker, halting a player who had just lost the opening rubber of the Davis Cup tie in Prague. Nakashima plays with the **Yonex VCORE 98** — <ProductRef id="yonex-vcore-98-2026" /> —, the spin-and-control frame he shares with Hurkacz and Rybakina, already in our catalog.

## Jódar, a statement debut: 6-2, 6-3 over Bublik

The performance of the day belonged to **Rafa Jódar, 20**: 6-2 6-3 in **1h25** against Alexander Bublik in his Laver Cup debut. The **8-0** run he put together between 2-2 in the first set and 4-0 in the second left the Kazakh without answers — Bublik finished Friday with a double defeat, also falling in the doubles. Jódar plays with the **HEAD Speed MP 2026** — <ProductRef id="head-speed-mp-2026" /> —, the all-rounder of the Speed line, the same family as Novak Djokovic.

## Alcaraz & Menšík close the day: 6-4, 6-4 over Fritz-Bublik

**Carlos Alcaraz** put the finishing touch in the first doubles of the edition: alongside Menšík, his Blade 98 v10 partner — <ProductRef id="wilson-blade-98-v10-2026" /> —, he beat **Taylor Fritz and Bublik** 6-4 6-4 (1h27) and restored Europe's two-point cushion. Mundo Deportivo reports that the Spaniard **will take the singles responsibility on Saturday**, a day worth two points per win in which Zverev — with his <ProductRef id="head-gravity-tour-zverev-2026" />, the golden edition he used to lift the US Open —, Cobolli, De Minaur and Tien are all yet to debut.
