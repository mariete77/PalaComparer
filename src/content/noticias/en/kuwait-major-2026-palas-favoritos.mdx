The Premier Padel tour lands in the Middle East this week for a tournament grown at the last minute: the **Kuwait Major**, played **October 25-31** at the **Rafa Nadal Academy Kuwait** with 2,000 points on offer for the champions. The men's main draw opened this Sunday with the first round; the women's starts Monday the 26th. And it does so with a storyline that changes everything: **Ale Galán and Fede Chingotto, the second seeds, have withdrawn due to the Madrid man's injury**, confirmed by Premier Padel just before the draw.

## A last-minute Major with a Major's prize money

The tournament was born as a P1 and **was elevated to Major status — the tour's top category — after the Doha event was postponed**. The upgrade is not cosmetic: the winners take **2,000 points** (double a P1) and the prize money pot reaches **€1,044,849**, according to the official FIP event page. The Rafa Nadal Academy Kuwait is no ordinary venue either: it already hosted the 2024 Kuwait P1 and the **2025 FIP World Cup Pairs**, the pairs World Championship won by none other than Galán and Chingotto. With 48 men's pairs and 40 women's pairs in the main draw, the schedule bites from day one: round of 16 on Wednesday the 28th, quarter-finals Thursday the 29th, semi-finals Friday the 30th and **the final on Saturday the 31st at 4 pm**, all on indoor courts — MejorSet structures, MONDO turf — with Bullpadel Next Pro balls.

## The withdrawal that changes everything

Galán-Chingotto's retirement strikes the battle for number one we analysed at [September's Madrid P1](/noticias/madrid-p1-2026-lucha-por-el-numero-uno): the world No. 2 pair needed to pile up points in Kuwait to close the gap and are left without the chance. The big beneficiaries are **Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia**, who arrive on a roll: **four consecutive titles** — Paris, Rotterdam, Düsseldorf and Milan — plus eleven trophies in 2026 and an overwhelming winning record this season. Their latest reference is the Allianz Cloud final: a week ago they came back on Lebrón-Augsburger **4-6 6-3 6-2** to seal [the Milan poker](/noticias/milano-p1-2026-campeones-tapia-coello-sanchez-ustero).

## The draw: Coello-Tapia's road runs through Lebrón

With Galán-Chingotto out, the top half becomes the hot zone: **Tapia-Coello (1)** and **Lebrón-Augsburger (3)** can only meet in the semi-finals, and the Spanish-Argentine pair arrive straight from the Milan final. The corridor also includes a potential quarter-final against **Paquito Navarro and Martín Di Nenno (5)**, with the **Coki Nieto-Mike Yanguas (6)** wall waiting in Coello's section before that. In the bottom half, **Stupaczuk-Jon Sanz (4)** inherit the favourites' role after the withdrawal, with **Guerrero-Leal (7)** as the direct rival of similar level.

## Women's draw: a throne up for grabs with 6,500 points on the table

The women's draw keeps all its tension: **Triay-Brea (1)** defend the leadership chased by **Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero (3)**, the in-form pair of the autumn after winning three of the last four events — Paris, Rotterdam and Milan — and cutting the gap to **1,980 points** with 6,500 still in play. In Milan they already took down the world No. 1s in the semis (6-1 6-4). **Josemaría-Bea González (2)** and the young **Claudia Fernández-Martina Calvo (4)** complete the favourites' pack. ## Schedule and where to watch

The men's first round plays this Sunday the 25th (2 pm) with the women's debut on Monday the 26th (12 pm); round of 16 Wednesday the 28th, quarter-finals Thursday the 29th, semi-finals Friday the 30th (2 pm) and the final on Saturday the 31st (4 pm), all at the Rafa Nadal Academy Kuwait. The tournament is broadcast live on **Red Bull TV**, which carries the Kuwait Major from start to finish.
