Before looking at the carbon, the core or whose name is on it, look at the shape.
It's what determines where the sweet spot sits, how much power the paddle hands
you and how much it punishes you when you miss the center.

## The three shapes (and a fourth)

### Round — sweet spot in the center

The balance sits low, close to your hand. That means the paddle handles itself:
you bring it up fast, defend comfortably and off-center hits don't fly off you.
In exchange, the smash doesn't come for free; you have to supply the power.

It's the default shape if you've been playing under two years, if you play a
backhand-heavy game, or if paddles feel heavy to you late in a match.

<ProductRef id="nox-ml10-pro-cup-2023" /> is the textbook example: round,
low balance and a soft rubber.

### Teardrop — the real middle ground

The sweet spot sits a little higher than on a round paddle and the balance is
medium. You get ball exit without losing much control. It's the best-selling
shape precisely because it doesn't force you to choose.

<ProductRef id="nox-at10-genius-18k-2026" /> is a premium teardrop: it hits
hard when you load it up, but still forgives.

### Diamond — everything up top

High balance, small sweet spot very close to the head. When you catch it clean,
the ball flies. When you don't, it dies in the net and you feel the tug in your
elbow.

<ProductRef id="bullpadel-vertex-05-2026" /> and
<ProductRef id="head-extreme-pro-2024" /> are pure diamonds: a smasher's
paddle for a player with built technique, not for someone still learning.

<Callout title="The short rule">
If you're torn between teardrop and diamond, take the teardrop. A paddle that
doesn't forgive doesn't make you a better player — it makes you miss more.
</Callout>

### Hybrid — the catch-all label

Brands call "hybrid" whatever falls between teardrop and diamond. In practice
you have to look at the actual balance, because very different paddles hide
under the same label. <ProductRef id="bullpadel-neuron-02-2026" /> is the
typical case: hybrid shape and medium balance, but with a dry, premium feel
that's nothing in-between.
