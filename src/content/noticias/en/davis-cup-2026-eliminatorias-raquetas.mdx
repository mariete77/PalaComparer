Four weeks after the US Open final, team tennis takes center stage: from **September 18 to 20** the **Davis Cup Qualifiers** are played — seven home-and-away ties (best of five rubbers, two singles on Friday, then doubles and two more singles on Saturday) whose winners reach the **Final 8 in Bologna** (November 24-29), where defending champions Italy are already waiting. And in three of the most anticipated ties — Prague, Quebec City and Halle — catalog racquets are on court. (Sources: daviscup.com, USTA, Tennis Canada.)

## Prague: Fritz-less USA storms the O2 Arena

Friday's main event is at Prague's **O2 Arena**, where the United States — 32 titles, but a **19-year wait for the next one** — face Czechia (USA lead the head-to-head 5-2, though the Czechs won the 2025 tie in Delray Beach). A late-round shake-up: **Taylor Fritz (No. 10) and Tommy Paul pulled out**, and captain Bob Bryan called up the two US Open revelations, **Learner Tien (No. 13, age 20)** and **Martin Damm (No. 104)**, who pushed Tiafoe to five sets in New York.

Day one brings two blockbusters: **Tien vs Jakub Mensik** — a rematch of their US Open fourth-round duel, won by the American 6-3, 1-6, 6-7(2), 6-3, 6-4 — and **Ben Shelton vs Jiri Lehecka**, five days after Shelton's Grand Slam final. The US Open runner-up plays the **Yonex EZONE 98** — <ProductRef id="yonex-ezone-98-2025" /> —: 98 sq in, 320 g and Yonex's enlarged sweet spot for the most electric serve on tour. In the Czech team, captained by Tomas Berdych, the third man is **Tomas Machac**, a fixture of the **Yonex VCORE family** — in our catalog, the [VCORE 100](/producto/yonex-vcore-100-2026) <ProductRef id="yonex-vcore-100-2026" />, the same line Rybakina and Paolini use. Alongside him, Mensik and Lehecka both carry Wilson frames (Blade and Six.One 95).

## Quebec City: the hometown hero, with Alcaraz and Rune's Pure Aero 98

Friday's other big ticket is **the first Davis Cup tie in Quebec City since 1975**: Canada host France at the **Centre Videotron** behind the ultimate local. **Felix Auger-Aliassime, world No. 5 and raised in the city**, plays his first professional matches at home and holds nothing back: Thursday's draw put him down for **both singles and doubles** (debuting as a partnership with Duncan Chan), with Liam Draxl as the second singles player after Gabriel Diallo withdrew.

France — Arthur Rinderknech (No. 32) and Quentin Halys in singles, Bonzi-Herbert in doubles — will face the weekend's flashiest gear stat: **FAA plays the 2026 Babolat Pure Aero 98** — <ProductRef id="babolat-pure-aero-98-2026" /> —, the same racquet as **Carlos Alcaraz and Holger Rune** (source: Babolat). A 98 sq-in frame with a 16x20 pattern and 323 g strung: control and spin for players who take the ball early. His player page, [felix-auger-aliassime](/jugadores/felix-auger-aliassime), is brand new in the catalog.

## Halle: Zverev, from the New York salad bowl to the Gravity

On Saturday the baton passes to **Germany vs Croatia in Halle** (indoor hard) with the man of the moment: **Alexander Zverev, world No. 2 and double Grand Slam champion in 2026** — Roland Garros and the US Open — leads the hosts alongside Hanfmann, Krawietz and Puetz. His racquet is already a catalog item: the **Head Gravity Tour Zverev 2026**, gold and purple, limited edition, with the first open 16x19 pattern in Gravity family history — <ProductRef id="head-gravity-tour-zverev-2026" /> —, the same frame he lifted the Arthur Ashe trophy with. Across the net: veteran **Marin Cilic** and the Pavic-Mektic doubles pair.

## Vienna: Blockx's TFight, Belgium's breakout star

Friday also opens **Austria vs Belgium on Vienna clay**, with the toughest head-to-head of the round (Austria lead 5-0). Belgium travel with their new star: **Alexander Blockx, US Open 2026 quarterfinalist** (he retired with a rib injury against Khachanov with a semi-final in sight) plays a **Tecnifibre TFight** — the same frame family as the catalog's [TFight 305S](/producto/tecnifibre-tfight-305s-2025) <ProductRef id="tecnifibre-tfight-305s-2025" />, Daniil Medvedev's racquet — and arrives in the best form of his 21-year-old career.

Meanwhile, **Spain (without its top players, led by Rafael Jodar, No. 11, and Landaluce) visits Chile in Santiago** on clay, and **Great Britain host Ecuador in London**.
